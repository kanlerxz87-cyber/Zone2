What the comparison actually shows

  • These are not interchangeable products. Two are basket air fryers and one is a 26-quart French-door oven. Capacity ranges from 6 qt to 26 qt.
  • All three are 1700 W on the manufacturer's or Amazon's own figures. The Chefman 8 Qt listing's bullet text claims 1000 W, which conflicts with the manufacturer — that conflict is flagged rather than resolved silently.
  • Power draw is not a differentiator here. All three sit at the top of what a standard US 120 V / 15 A kitchen circuit handles comfortably, and none should share a circuit with another high-draw appliance.
  • Capacity, not wattage, is the real choice. Two small independent baskets solve "main and side at once"; one large cavity solves "a whole chicken or a 12-inch pizza".

The bullets above are direct readings of the sourced rows in the table. No performance claim is made, because none of the three products was tested by this project.
