## How to decide

1. Read `pages/suggestions.html`. Each row states the options, which one is recommended, the trade-off, and what must be answered first.
2. Answer the blocking questions on `pages/questions.html`. Blank means unknown; nothing there is guessed.
3. Record one decision per suggestion in `ledger/suggestions.json`, then regenerate with `python3 tools/build_site.py && python3 tools/build_decisions.py`.
4. Implement only what was adopted, one batch per decision, each with its own gate run.

## Compliance position, stated plainly

The site is compliant on the things that can be checked mechanically, and the gates prove it: the required disclosure sentence is on every page, no product price is published anywhere, outbound product links are qualified with `rel="sponsored"`, and no rating or review figure is displayed without a licensed feed. It is not yet monetized and not yet author-attributed, and both are recorded as gaps rather than described as complete.
