*Sources.* S47, S02

### SG-16 — Publication polish (reader capture)

**Decide the reader-capture shape before any address is collected**

*Proposed.* The collector's /subscribe endpoint exists and stores exactly an address, the page and the day - nothing else. Decide whether reader capture runs through that first-party endpoint (owner deploys the sink, MI-01), through a third-party form provider (which needs a privacy review of its own under PIPEDA, M-08/EV-05 rows S31/S32), or not at all. Nothing collects addresses until the owner chooses and the sink is deployed.

*Recommended option.* A — First-party /subscribe on the owner's deployed sink, double opt-in handled by the owner's list tooling

*Why.* The brief names reader capture among the publication passes. Collecting an address is collecting personal information, so the shape of the capture must be decided before the first one arrives - not after. The minimal option is already written and tested: the endpoint stores three fields, sends no email itself, and the published pages carry no form until the owner picks.

*Sources.* S31, S32, S49

- **A** First-party /subscribe on the owner's deployed sink, double opt-in handled by the owner's list tooling — The data stays on hardware the owner controls; the owner runs a sink and a confirmation step.
- **B** Third-party form provider embedded on the pages — Less to operate, but the provider's processing needs its own privacy review and disclosure before it ships.
- **C** No reader capture — No personal information to protect and no list to grow; the /subscribe code stays dormant.

## How to decide

1. Read `pages/suggestions.html`. Each row states the options, which one is recommended, the trade-off, and what must be answered first.
2. Answer the blocking questions on `pages/questions.html`. Blank means unknown; nothing there is guessed.
3. Record one decision per suggestion in `ledger/suggestions.json`, then regenerate with `python3 tools/build_site.py && python3 tools/build_decisions.py`.
4. Implement only what was adopted, one batch per decision, each with its own gate run.
