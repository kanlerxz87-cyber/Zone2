Analysis workflow

  1. Pricing analysis — current price and price history tracked from official Amazon listings; comparison against category benchmarks.
  2. Review analysis — rating and review volume from the official product listing, plus sentiment review of customer reviews.
  3. Competition analysis — number and strength of competing listings and affiliates in the same category.
  4. Commission potential — estimated commission per qualifying sale computed from Qualifying Revenue and the official rate in the Commission Income Statement Appendix.
  5. Automation — the pipeline re-runs these checks continuously and re-publishes findings with per-row source links.
