Disclosure requirements — applied on this site

To meet the Associate Program's requirements, you must (1) include a legally compliant disclosure with your links and (2) identify yourself on your Site as an Amazon Associate with the language required by the Operating Agreement. Verified

Per the official help topic, to comply with FTC regulations the link-level disclosure must be:

  1. Clear — e.g., (paid link), #ad, or #CommissionsEarned.
  2. Conspicuous — placed near any affiliate link or product review, in a location customers will notice easily.

In addition, the Operating Agreement requires that the following statement clearly and conspicuously appears on your Site:

“As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases.”

How the platform enforces compliance

  • Verification-first content — content is generated only for products verified against official Amazon sources, preventing unverified claims.
  • Special Links only — affiliate links use the tagged Special Link formats from the user's own Associates account; the platform never stores customer account data.
  • Disclosure injection — the required Associate statement appears in the site footer on every page, with link-level disclosure near affiliate content.
  • No click manipulation — no artificial clicks, redirects, or in-app framing; links open the Amazon Site only after a real user click.
