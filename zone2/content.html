SEO & compliance rules for generated content

  1. Every factual claim in generated content links back to an official source.
  2. No claim is stated for a product whose identifier cannot be confirmed from an official source.
  3. Affiliate links are used only on Special Links generated through the user's own Associates account.
  4. Clear, conspicuous disclosure is placed near every affiliate link and product recommendation, per the Compliance page.
