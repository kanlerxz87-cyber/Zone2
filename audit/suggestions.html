R-05 · Decide whether the site measures anything at all
blocked on information

Proposal. Pick one measurement posture and publish it. The site currently describes tracking, conversion analysis and campaign optimization in detail while measuring nothing.

Why. Until an Associates tag exists (SA-03), a click count cannot be tied to a commission, so the cheapest correct posture is to measure nothing and say so. Option B becomes worthwhile on the day the tag exists. (finding A-05)

  • A. No measurement until an Associates account exists recommended
    Zero privacy surface, zero data. The optimization pages become explicitly 'planned' rather than 'available'.
  • B. First-party click counter on a host the owner controls
    Real outbound-click counts, no cookies or visitor ids (the schema in tools/tracking_schema.sql already assumes this). Needs hosting and a privacy statement.
  • C. Third-party analytics
    Fastest to read, largest privacy and consent surface, and it introduces the only third-party script on the site.
Costestimate: A is copy-only; B is one session plus hosting
Riskestimate: A none; B low if the no-visitor-identifier rule is kept
