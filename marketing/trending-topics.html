How these topics earn organic traffic

  • Answer a specific question. Google surfaces pages that directly answer long-tail queries ("can you cook a whole chicken in a 26-qt air fryer?"), not generic roundups.
  • Lead with verified numbers. Capacity, wattage, temperature range and presets come from manufacturer pages — specific figures outperform vague adjectives.
  • Compare, don't just describe. Head-to-head angles (dual-basket vs single-basket; large vs compact; air fryer vs oven) match comparison shoppers who are closer to a purchase decision.
  • Time it to demand. Seasonal angles (holiday cooking, back-to-school, gift guides) ride real search spikes instead of flat evergreen content.
  • Link everything together. Articles link to comparisons and the buying guide; comparisons link back to articles. Internal links spread authority across the set.
  • Disclose plainly. Every product link carries rel="sponsored" and a "(paid link)" note — clear to readers and compliant with the FTC and Google.
