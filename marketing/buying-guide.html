Which air fryer, for which kitchen

A buying guide that only makes recommendations the specifications can support. Every pick below is decided by a number printed on a manufacturer page, and that page is linked beside it.

The picks

  • Most capacity: EMERIL LAGASSE FRENCH DOOR AIRFRYER 360™ TOASTER OVEN COMBO, 26 QT — 26 Qt / 24.6 L (M3 · www.emerileveryday.commanufacturer)
  • Most compact basket: TurboFry® Touch 6 Qt. Dual-Window Air Fryer — 6 Qt / 5.67 L (M1 · chefman.commanufacturer)
  • Widest stated temperature range: TurboFry® Touch 6 Qt. Dual-Window Air Fryer — 200–450 °F / 95–235 °C (M1 · chefman.commanufacturer)

Capacity and temperature ranges are compared as the manufacturer states them. Air-fryer capacity claims are not directly interchangeable between a two-basket unit and a single-basket unit, so the guide explains what each figure actually measures rather than declaring one "bigger".

What this guide will not tell you

  • What they cost. Prices change by the hour and the Associates operating rules restrict how they may be displayed. Nothing here quotes a price; check the listing.
  • How well they cook. No member of this project has tested these units. Claims about crispness or speed are the manufacturers', and are attributed to them.
  • How buyers rate them. Star ratings differ by Amazon storefront and move daily, so no rating is printed as a fact.
  • Which one to buy. The numbers decide between them for a stated use. Where the numbers do not separate them — notably where two official pages contradict each other — this guide says so instead of manufacturing a winner.
