Hi-Fry, 450 F and 400 F: why two official pages disagree about one fryer

On one product, Amazon's title says the Hi-Fry option reaches 450 F. The manufacturer's own page for the same model states a range that stops at 400 F. This is not a typo you can pick a side of. It is a difference between a listing written for a category and a specification written for a machine - and it is exactly the kind of thing a buying decision should be made around, not through.

Why this exists: The two statements are both verbatim from official pages, we quote both, and we publish the number the manufacturer states while flagging the listing. Very few affiliate posts will show you their disagreement.

What Hi-Fry is, on the page that defines it

On the 6 Qt dual-window page, Chefman lists a maximum temperature of 450 F / 235 C with the Hi-Fry function. So the function is a temperature ceiling - an above-normal setting for a finishing stage, not a separate cooking mode. The same brand's 8 Qt page states its range as 200-400 F, with no Hi-Fry figure printed in the specification block.M1 · chefman.commanufacturerM5 · chefman.commanufacturer

Where the 450 F on the listing comes from

We do not know, and we are not going to guess. What is verifiable: the Amazon title for the 8 Qt reads TurboFry 4-in-1 with 450 F Hi-Fry Option for Crispier Results, while the manufacturer's specification for that model states 200-400 F. One of the two is describing a different variant, and neither page says which.M6 · www.amazon.comAmazon listingM5 · chefman.commanufacturer

Our take — an editorial judgement, not a claim the manufacturer makes

For the 8 Qt we publish 400 F, because a specification block outranks a search title, and a listing written by a reseller is not a manufacturer spec. For the 6 Qt we publish 450 F, because that is what the manufacturer's own feature list states for that model. If you need a Hi-Fry setting on the counter, buy the model whose own page prints it.

What this means for a shopping list

Treat a temperature in a product title as a claim to be checked against the specification, on this brand and on every brand. Where the two disagree, we mark the row CONFLICT in the tables on this site rather than quietly choosing one number - the same row you are reading is the one that took us a day to settle.M5 · chefman.commanufacturerM6 · www.amazon.comAmazon listing
