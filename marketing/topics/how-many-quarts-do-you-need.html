How many quarts of air fryer do you actually need?

Capacity is the one number every buyer looks at and almost nobody can translate. Six quarts of what? Here is the only honest way to answer it: read what each manufacturer says its own basket is sized for, and then do the arithmetic on the dimensions, because the quarts figure is measured by the brand and no two measure it the same way.

Why this exists: Every competitor answers this with a rule of thumb for everybody. We answer it for these three specific models, with the published servings claim beside the internal dimensions, and we mark the rows where the brand never published a servings figure at all.

What the three pages actually claim

Two of the three manufacturers put a household size next to the capacity. The third puts nothing: its page states a quart figure and stops. That absence is the most useful thing on the page, because it tells you who is expected to cook in it.M1 · chefman.commanufacturerM3 · www.emerileveryday.commanufacturerM5 · chefman.commanufacturer

The 6 Qt dual-basket model is the only one of the three that never publishes a servings number on its product page. Chefman publishes instead two independent 3 Qt baskets and a Sync Finish function that ends both baskets at the same moment - which is a different answer to the same question: this is a machine for cooking two things at once, not a machine for cooking a lot of one thing.M1 · chefman.commanufacturerM2 · www.amazon.comAmazon listing

Use the box, not the badge

Quarts are measured by the brand. Dimensions are not negotiable: 13.38 x 11.81 x 10.63 in is the space the 6 Qt unit needs, and 15.8 in across is the width of the Emeril. If the cabinet above your counter is 14 in of clearance, the tall fryer oven is out no matter what its capacity claim says.M1 · chefman.commanufacturerM3 · www.emerileveryday.commanufacturer

Our take — an editorial judgement, not a claim the manufacturer makes

Measure your counter depth and the height of the lowest obstruction above it before you compare any two capacity figures. Then compare capacity. On all three of these pages, the footprint number is the one that will make the decision for you.

One thing none of the three publishes: a minimum clearance requirement for ventilation. That row is blank on our tables and it is not a secret - the information is simply not on the official product pages. If the clearance number matters in your kitchen, it comes from the instruction manual, which is a document we have not been given and will not quote from memory.M1 · chefman.commanufacturerM3 · www.emerileveryday.commanufacturerM5 · chefman.commanufacturer

Rule of thumb, for what it is worth

  • One or two people, mostly reheating: the smallest of these is enough, and a second basket buys you nothing.
  • Four to six people: both the 8 Qt basket and the 26 Qt oven publish that household size, and they reach it by opposite routes - one big batch, or an oven that roasts.
  • Two dishes that finish together: only the dual-basket model publishes that function, and it is the reason that design exists.
