  • Category · commercial investigation

    Air fryer or air fryer toaster oven: the door is the decision

    Basket, or oven with doors? Most comparisons answer with a list of extra modes. That is the wrong first question. The mechanical difference is the door: a basket you pull out, or a front that swings open so a sheet pan can slide in. Everything else follows from that, including which of them can roast a chicken.

    Hi-Fry, 450 F and 400 F: why two official pages disagree about one fryer

    On one product, Amazon's title says the Hi-Fry option reaches 450 F. The manufacturer's own page for the same model states a range that stops at 400 F. This is not a typo you can pick a side of. It is a difference between a listing written for a category and a specification written for a machine - and it is exactly the kind of thing a buying decision should be made around, not through.

    24 presets or 25? A field guide to spec-sheet drift on appliance pages

The model number on the store page ends in 02A; the one in the Amazon specification block ends in 02X. This is not a scandal - it is normal, it is everywhere, and once you can see it you cannot shop the same way again.
