Questions to ask before buying a multi-function kitchen appliance

A machine that promises ten jobs invites one question more than any other: which of those jobs does its own page actually document? This is a checklist built the only honest way we know - from what the manufacturer and the listing state in writing, including the places they contradict each other. Nothing here is a popularity claim; it is a reading of the pages.

Why this exists: Every one of these questions is answerable from the documents a seller already publishes. Where the documents disagree, the question becomes one to ask the seller before paying - and we show the disagreement rather than picking a side.

What does multi-function actually cover on this machine's page?

The 26 QT French-door model's manufacturer page names its modes in full: a 10-in-1 list of Air Fry, Bake, Broil, Defrost, Dehydrate, Grill, Proof, Reheat, Roast and Warm. That is the list to work from - a badge like 'multi-function' names nothing, while a page that itemises its modes has told you exactly what it claims to do.M3 · www.emerileveryday.commanufacturer

  • Which of these named modes will I use in a normal week - and which need an accessory to work at all?
  • Does any mode depend on the rotisserie spit or another part that could be lost or extra?
  • Is the mode list on the page I am buying from the same as the one on the brand's page?

How many presets does it have, according to which page?

On this model the official pages do not agree with each other. The manufacturer's spec tile reads '25 Cooking Pre-sets', the same site's own product description reads '24 one-touch cooking presets', and the Amazon title reads '24 cooking functions'. The count is therefore not settled - and a page that contradicts itself about its own headline number is a page whose finer print deserves a direct question to the seller before purchase.M3 · www.emerileveryday.commanufacturerM4 · www.amazon.comAmazon listing

Our take — an editorial judgement, not a claim the manufacturer makes

A preset count is the least important number on the page and the easiest to inflate. The disagreement here matters less as a defect than as a signal: ask the seller which number is current, and make sure the answer arrives in writing.

What does it displace on the counter?

Multi-function usually means one machine standing in for several, and the space question is concrete. This model's manufacturer states its dimensions as 16.9 inches deep by 15.8 inches wide by 14.8 inches high; the two basket models in this set are smaller machines - the 6 Qt dual-basket measures 13.38 by 11.81 by 10.63 inches and the 8 Qt measures 15.4 by 12.6 by 13.5 inches. Measure the shelf it will live on, including the door swing the French doors describe.M3 · www.emerileveryday.commanufacturerM1 · chefman.commanufacturerM5 · chefman.commanufacturer

  • What clearance does the manufacturer require behind and above the unit?
  • Does the door need room to open fully - and which way do the doors face?
  • If it replaces two appliances, where do those go instead?

What actually comes in the box?

This is where the pages disagree most sharply. The manufacturer's description says 'Included are 7 premium accessories' and names three of them - an air fry basket, a rotisserie spit and a bake pan - without itemising the rest. The Amazon title for the same model says 'Six Cooking Accessories Included', while Amazon's own item-details block describes a configuration 'with 24 Functions and 7 Accessories' but names only six components. Two official pages, three different statements, and no published answer.M3 · www.emerileveryday.commanufacturerM4 · www.amazon.comAmazon listing

Our take — an editorial judgement, not a claim the manufacturer makes

Ask for the accessory list in writing before paying. A part that is promised in prose but missing from the box is a claim you can only settle one way: get the enumeration, not the count.

What does the warranty cover, and who honours it?

The manufacturer states a 1-year warranty for the French-door model; the two Chefman models in this set carry what their manufacturer calls a 1-year assurance. Warranty words vary between brands, and which page states the term matters: the page you buy from should repeat what the brand's page promises.M3 · www.emerileveryday.commanufacturerM1 · chefman.commanufacturerM5 · chefman.commanufacturer

  • Is the warranty term stated on the listing I am buying from, or only on the brand's site?
  • Who pays shipping if the unit needs service - and is service local?
  • What voids it: which cleaning method does the manufacturer actually permit?

Two more questions the page cannot answer for you

Capacity claims on this model are stated as examples - the manufacturer says it is sized to fit a whole chicken, 12-inch pizzas or a full tray of cookies, and the spec tile describes food for 4-6 people. Whether that matches your household is a question no spec sheet can settle. And price is deliberately absent from this site: prices on the listing change without notice, so we leave the number to the page you are reading.M3 · www.emerileveryday.commanufacturer

  • Does 'food for 4-6 people' match the meals I actually cook - and does one tray cook them at once?
  • What is today's price on the listing itself, including any delivery terms I should know about?
