Air fryer or air fryer toaster oven: the door is the decision

Basket, or oven with doors? Most comparisons answer with a list of extra modes. That is the wrong first question. The mechanical difference is the door: a basket you pull out, or a front that swings open so a sheet pan can slide in. Everything else follows from that, including which of them can roast a chicken.

Why this exists: The Emeril page is unusually explicit about its door and its 26-quart interior, which lets us tie the mode list to the geometry instead of reciting it.

The door, because it decides everything else

The Emeril page describes dual French doors that open for access to a 26 Qt chamber, sized on the manufacturer's own tile for preparing food for four to six people, with a dual-speed fan for crisping and 360-degree hot-air circulation. A basket fryer cannot offer that geometry: the food sits in a drawer, and the drawer sets the maximum size of what fits.M3 · www.emerileveryday.commanufacturer

The basket model's counter-argument is also on its page, and it is a good one: two independent baskets, a viewing window on each, an interior light, and a 450 F top temperature.M1 · chefman.commanufacturer

Ten modes, and what the list is really for

The Emeril lists ten functions on its page: Air Fry, Bake, Broil, Defrost, Dehydrate, Grill, Proof, Reheat, Roast, Warm. Read as a sentence it is marketing; read as a set of racks and elements it is a specification. Bake, Broil and Roast need chamber height. Dehydrate and Proof need a low end of the temperature scale. The basket fryers here publish a range starting at 200 F; the oven publishes one starting at 75 F.M3 · www.emerileveryday.commanufacturerM1 · chefman.commanufacturerM5 · chefman.commanufacturer

Our take — an editorial judgement, not a claim the manufacturer makes

If you want one appliance to replace a second oven, the low end and the height of the chamber matter more than how many verbs the box prints. If you want crispy frozen food fast, a basket reaches temperature with less empty air to heat and a third of the counter.
