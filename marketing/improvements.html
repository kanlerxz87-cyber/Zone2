Review before anything else changes

The improvement board

Every open suggestion for this site, with the options, what each one costs, and the answer it is waiting for. 8 of 14 are already built the recommended way and can be reversed here; the rest are not implemented, on purpose - a suggestion that ships before the owner reads it is not a suggestion.

Nothing on this page is a program fact. Recommendations are judgements built on the official sources cited in each entry; the facts they rest on are on the verification page.

How to decide

  1. Read the index below. Choose the ones worth a decision today - the rest stay open and cost nothing.
  2. For each, either record a choice in the decision field of marketing/data/editorial.json, or reply with the IDs and letters. Both are enough.
  3. An entry whose question is unanswered stays blocked. The blocking IDs are named on the card, and answering the question is usually cheaper than the decision it gates.
  4. Programme-level decisions (account, marketplace, niche, monetisation) live on the owner dashboard's decision board and question register. This board is the site: design, copy, traffic and measurement. They cross-reference and do not overlap.

DS-01 — Keep the anti-slop rule set as a machine-checked gate, not an aspiration

implemented as recommended

The ten voice rules in this file are enforced by the marketing gate: the build fails on a banned phrase, on a factual sentence with no source, and on an unlabelled opinion. Extend the banned-phrase list from real rejects rather than from a generic list.

Why: Style advice that is not enforced decays within one regeneration. The gate is what keeps the next session from restoring the house style nobody wanted.

Options

  • AGate the rule set in the build (done in this pass)Some false positives on legitimate phrasing; each one needs a judgement call, not a workaround.recommended
  • BPublish the rule set as guidance for writers onlyNothing stops drift; the site returns to generic copy at the next regeneration.
  • CManual editorial review per articleHighest quality ceiling, needs a human on every publish; contradicts the automation brief.

DS-05 — State what we did not do, once, in plain language

implemented as recommended

Every article carries a short What we did not do box: nobody cooked with these machines; we read the official pages on a date. Keep it at one sentence, in the reader's language, not in legal register.

Why: The site's whole competitive position is that it does not pretend. A single honest sentence buys more credibility than any amount of praise, and it keeps the affiliate disclosure from reading like the only honest thing on the page.

Options

  • AOne sentence per article (done)Some readers will discount the recommendation. They are the ones who would have discounted it anyway.recommended
  • BOne method page, no per-article noteCleaner pages; the caveat becomes something a reader has to go looking for.
  • CSay nothing about methodReads like every other affiliate page, and is the option that makes the site sound generated.

DS-07 — Do not add Product or Review markup while there is no genuine review

implemented as recommended

The build emits Article and BreadcrumbList markup only. Product/Review/AggregateRating markup stays out until a real, first-hand review exists and the reviews it describes are on the same page.

Why: Google's review-snippet documentation carries a self-serving restriction and requires ratings to come from users, and a rich-result promise we cannot keep is a manual-action risk on a domain we intend to keep. Article and BreadcrumbList are safe today.

Options

  • AArticle + Breadcrumb only (done)No stars in the snippet, and no eligibility risk.recommended
  • BAdd Product markup nowProduct snippets require review, aggregateRating or offers - so we would be shipping an empty or borrowed rating.
  • CAdd markup only after a test panel existsBest long-term; needs Q-11 answered with a dated record.

awaiting your decision

Every page on this site carries a verified-on date. Either commit to a cadence that re-reads the official pages before the date goes stale, or stop dating the pages. A dated page nobody re-checks is worse than an undated one.

The writing standard, enforced by the build

Write like a specialist who has read every page of the manual and is telling a friend what to buy: short sentences, real numbers, an opinion, and no adjectives doing work a number could do.

Ten rules. The marketing gate checks the banned-phrase list and requires every factual sentence to carry a source, so a regeneration cannot quietly restore the house style.
IDRuleWhat that looks likeWhat it replacesWhy
V-01Open with the decision, not with the categoryHow many quarts you need depends on how many people you cook for - and on whether you reheat or roast. Here is the arithmetic on all three spec sheets.In today's fast-paced kitchen, air fryers have become an essential appliance for modern home cooks.Generic scene-setting is the single strongest AI tell, and it is also the paragraph a reader skips. Google's own guidance is to publish non-commodity content and not to recycle what a generative model could produce.
V-02Every factual sentence carries its sourceThe 6 Qt model runs to 450 degrees F in Hi-Fry (Chefman spec page).It gets hotter than most rivals for maximum crispiness.A claim with no source is either a guess or marketing copy. On this site a sentence with a number is a row in a table, and the table row ends in the page it came from.
V-03Separate the measurement from the judgement, visiblyPut the opinion in a labelled box, and say what it rests on.Naturally, this is the best choice for families.Readers can forgive a wrong opinion. They cannot forgive an opinion presented as a spec. The gate checks the label is present, so an unlabelled judgement fails the build.
V-04No superlatives, no hype verbsIt is the widest of the three: 15.8 in across.This groundbreaking, game-changing appliance revolutionises your cooking.Superlatives are unfalsifiable and are the second strongest AI tell. A measurement is falsifiable, so it is worth more to a reader and to search.
V-05Name the number the brand did not publishNeither official page states an inner-basket material or a clearance requirement, so those rows are blank - not guessed.Silence, or a plausible filler value.Admitting a gap is the cheapest trust signal available and the one almost no affiliate site uses. V-06One sentence, one idea; average under 20 wordsBoth baskets finish together. The design prompt, as it should read next time

Feed this block to whoever designs the next revision. It is written so a model cannot quietly reintroduce the habits that made the previous version read as generated. Every constraint below is either an editorial standard or a rule the programme already imposes on this site.

Role

You are designing a niche consumer publication that earns affiliate revenue, not a dashboard and not a landing page for a tool.

Audience

A person one decision away from buying an air fryer, arriving from a question, in a hurry, on a phone, sceptical of praise.

Job of the page

Move the reader from a question to a decision in one scroll, then hand them to the listing. Everything else on the page exists to make that decision trustworthy.

Voice

Short declarative sentences. Numbers where adjectives would go. A stated opinion, in a labelled box, separated from the measurements. No hedging in circles: state a limit once, plainly, then move on.

Prose to refuse

Scene-setting openers, category eulogies, whether you are a X or a Y, three-card hero rows, and any sentence that would survive being pasted into an article about a kettle. The banned list is enforced by the build, and the list grows from rejects, not from taste.

Proof layout

Every factual sentence carries a citation that resolves to a manufacturer page or an Amazon listing. A blank row shows the reason for the blank. A disagreement between two sources is shown as a disagreement, with both, and states which number we use.

Visual system

Paper white, ink black, one rust accent, hairline rules, a serif for article titles, tabular numerals, no photography, no shadows, no gradients, no icons in circles, no carousels, no modals, no newsletter popup.

Compliance rails

The disclosure sentence on every page, a clear paid-link marker beside every product link, rel=sponsored on every outbound marketplace link, no price and no star rating anywhere, and no Amazon mark in our own naming. These are enforced by the deploy gate, not by memory.

Information architecture

A hub per cluster; guides that answer one search phrasing each; a per-model write-up; one comparison table that is the same data in a different shape. Every guide links to two others and to the buying guide. Sitemap and canonical tags on, Article and BreadcrumbList markup only.

Measurement

One static sub-tag per article once an Associates ID exists, so the outcome is visible per page. No user-derived tagging.

Definition of done

A reader who has never heard of the site can name the model that fits their kitchen and say why. If the page could be about any brand, it is not finished.
