Chefman · Air Fryers

CHEFMAN® 8 Qt. TurboFry® Touch Air Fryer

The largest single chamber of the three at 8 Qt, on the same 1700 W as its smaller sibling, with a four-preset panel that asks nothing of you. Its published range stops at 400 F, which is the one number worth arguing about, because the Amazon title for the same model advertises 450 F.

Model RJ38-SQSS-8T · ASIN B08DKYBTPH · read off chefman.com / www.amazon.com on 2026-11-14

Who it is for

Buy it if

  • You cook one large batch for four to six people, which the page states as the design target.
  • You want the shortest decision available: four presets, an LED shake reminder, and a documented auto shut-off.
  • The basket is going in the dishwasher - the page says basket and rack, top rack.

Skip it if

  • You need a 450 F finishing stage. The manufacturer specification for this model states 200-400 F.
  • You need to watch the food. No window is claimed on this page.
