Air Fryer Safety: What the USDA Says You Should Never Put in One

Raw stuffed chicken breast is the one hard prohibition. Everything else follows from the same two rules: follow the package, and check the temperature.

Published 2026-11-15 · 7 min read · Topic: Using an air fryer safely

Short answer

The USDA's Food Safety and Inspection Service publishes exactly one hard prohibition for air fryers: do not cook raw, stuffed, breaded chicken breast products in one. Everything else it asks for is about the thermometer and the clock rather than about a particular food.

The one thing the USDA tells you not to cook

The Food Safety and Inspection Service is explicit about one category: for raw, stuffed, breaded chicken breast products, the instruction is not to cook them in the air fryer, and to follow the manufacturer's cooking instructions instead.

That is the only food the published guidance rules out by name. It is worth knowing because it is easy to assume a breaded fillet behaves like a plain chicken breast, and the guidance says it does not.

S41 · www.fsis.usda.govUS government

Do not fill the basket

Air fryers do not have a lot of room inside. The published guidance is that overcrowding the appliance can prevent enough air circulation to properly cook the food, and that when air frying a large amount it may be best to do so in batches rather than all at once.

This is the single most common way an air fryer goes wrong, and it is a capacity problem before it is a timing problem. A basket that is packed is a basket with cold spots in it.

S41 · www.fsis.usda.govUS government

The starting numbers: 5 to 25 minutes, 350 to 400 degrees

The published range is that foods generally require 5 to 25 minutes of cooking at a temperature range of 350 degrees F to 400 degrees F. That is a starting window, not a recipe.

Where a package states its own instructions, the guidance is to follow them, and to always check that food has reached a safe minimum internal temperature with a food thermometer.

S41 · www.fsis.usda.govUS government

The four steps, in order

The guidance is organised as four steps. Clean: wash hands with soap and water for 20 seconds before and after preparing food, and clean and sanitize surfaces. Separate: keep cooked food off the plate that held raw food, and use separate cutting boards.

Cook: do not overfill the basket, and cook every air-fried food to a safe minimum internal temperature, measured with a food thermometer. Chill: refrigerate cooked food within 2 hours, or it enters the Danger Zone.

S41 · www.fsis.usda.govUS government

The two-hour window and the Danger Zone

Leftover foods should be refrigerated within 2 hours. The Danger Zone is the range of temperatures between 40 degrees F and 140 degrees F where foodborne illness bacteria multiply and can reach dangerous levels.

An air fryer makes this easier than a conventional oven in one specific way: cooking is fast, so the gap between the food coming out and the food going into the fridge can be short.

S41 · www.fsis.usda.govUS government

Why a thermometer beats a timer

This is the part that surprises people who have only read recipes. Each air fryer has varying cooking times due to the size and power of the appliance — but the safe internal temperature is always the same.

So a timer tells you when the recipe's suggestion has elapsed. A thermometer tells you whether the food is safe. They are different questions, and only one of them is the one that matters.

Questions people actually ask

What temperature should I cook chicken in an air fryer?
Poultry, including ground poultry, is safe to eat once it has reached an internal temperature of 165 degrees F (74 °C).
S41 · www.fsis.usda.govUS government S42 · www.foodsafety.govUS government
Can I air fry frozen chicken?
The published guidance does not publish a separate rule for frozen food. What it does publish is the rule that applies to every air-fried food: follow the manufacturer's instructions on the food package when they are available, and check the safe minimum internal temperature with a food thermometer.
S41 · www.fsis.usda.govUS government
How long can cooked food sit out?
Leftover foods should be refrigerated within 2 hours. The Danger Zone is the range between 40 degrees F and 140 degrees F, where foodborne illness bacteria multiply and can reach dangerous levels.
S41 · www.fsis.usda.govUS government
Do I need to preheat the air fryer?
Not addressed by the published guidance, so this site does not answer it either. The guidance it does publish is that foods generally require 5 to 25 minutes at 350–400 °F, and that the package instructions take precedence when they are available.
S41 · www.fsis.usda.govUS government
Is a timer enough?
No. Each air fryer has varying cooking times due to the size and power of the appliance, but the safe internal temperature is always the same, so the thermometer is the instrument that matters.
