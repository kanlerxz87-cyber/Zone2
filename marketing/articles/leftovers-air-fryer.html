Reheating Leftovers in an Air Fryer: The 165 °F Rule and the Two-Hour Window

Air fryers reheat well and the guidance is short: 165 °F, refrigerate within two hours, and watch the Danger Zone.

Published 2026-11-15 · 5 min read · Topic: Using an air fryer safely

Short answer

Reheat leftovers to 165 °F (74 °C) and refrigerate cooked food within 2 hours. Air fryers can be used for reheating food without the food becoming soggy, so the same appliance that cooked it can finish it.

Reheating is its own safety question

Leftovers carry their own number in the published chart: any type, 165 °F (74 °C). It is a separate line rather than a repeat of the original cooking temperature, because reheating a cooled-and-stored meal is not the same act as cooking it.

Precooked ham being reheated is also 165 °F, with the note that cooked hams packaged in USDA-inspected plants should be reheated to 140 °F (60 °C).

S42 · www.foodsafety.govUS government

The two-hour window starts when you take it out

Leftover foods should be refrigerated within 2 hours, otherwise they can enter the Danger Zone. The Danger Zone is the range of temperatures between 40 degrees F and 140 degrees F where foodborne illness bacteria multiply and can reach dangerous levels.

Air frying makes the window easier to keep, because cooking is quick. That advantage disappears if the food sits on the counter while somebody finishes a conversation.

S41 · www.fsis.usda.govUS government

Why an air fryer is a good reheat box

Air fryers can be used for baking, roasting and reheating food without the food becoming soggy. That is the whole argument for reheating in the same appliance rather than reaching for the microwave.

The 8 Qt model in particular is described by its manufacturer as having an automatic shutoff that halts cooking when the timer runs out or the basket is opened, and a top-rack dishwasher-safe basket and rack for the cleanup afterwards.

Rest time applies to some reheated foods too

Some meats need rest time after cooking, because rest time allows the innermost parts and juices of the meats to become fully and safely cooked. Whole cuts of beef, pork, veal and lamb and raw ham all carry a 3-minute rest at 145 °F.

That is a note about the food, not the appliance: no air fryer setting changes it.

S42 · www.foodsafety.govUS government

The appliance does not change the number

Each air fryer will have varying cooking times due to the size and power of the appliance, but the safe internal temperature is always the same. Use a food thermometer to make sure your meat and poultry is fully cooked.

A bigger oven does not get a bigger safety margin. That is a useful thing to remember when a specification sheet is trying to imply that a more expensive machine is the safer one.

Questions people actually ask

What temperature do I reheat leftovers to?
165 °F (74 °C). Leftovers of any type carry that number in the published chart.

How long can leftovers sit out?
