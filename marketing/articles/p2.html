Emeril Lagasse · Air Fryers

EMERIL LAGASSE FRENCH DOOR AIRFRYER 360™ TOASTER OVEN COMBO, 26 QT

A small convection oven that also air fries, and the door is the reason to buy it. French doors open onto a 26 Qt chamber 16.9 in deep with a published 75-500 F range: low enough to dehydrate, high enough to roast. A basket fryer cannot take a sheet pan. This can, and that is the counter space worth paying for.

Model 4-00675-02A-VN · ASIN B09B7SB46R · read off www.emerileveryday.com / www.amazon.com on 2026-11-14

Who it is for

Buy it if

  • You cook for four to six people, which is what the manufacturer's own capacity tile states.
  • You want one appliance to stand in for a second oven: ten published modes, proof through broil.
  • You have 16.9 in of counter depth and 14.8 in of height to give.
  • You want rotisserie without a second appliance: the manufacturer names a rotisserie spit among the seven accessories it counts in the box.

Skip it if

  • Your real need is a fast reheat. A basket heats far less empty air.
  • You cannot give up the counter permanently: at 14.8 in tall, this is a machine you move with intent.
