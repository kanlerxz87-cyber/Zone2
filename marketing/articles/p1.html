Chefman · Air Fryers

TurboFry® Touch 6 Qt. Dual-Window Air Fryer

Two baskets, not one big one. The published argument for this machine is a function rather than a volume: Sync Finish ends both 3 Qt baskets at the same moment, and the top of its range is 450 F in Hi-Fry. If your problem is two dishes on one counter, that is the specification to shop for. If your problem is one large batch, the 8 Qt model has the bigger chamber.

Model RJ38-SQPF-3TDB-2W · ASIN B0CDY277D3 · read off chefman.com / www.amazon.com on 2026-11-14

Who it is for

Buy it if

  • You cook two different things at once and want them on the table together.
  • You want to see the food without pulling a drawer: the manufacturer prints dual viewing windows and an interior light.
  • Your shelf clearance is tight. At 10.63 in tall it is the shortest of the three.

Skip it if

  • You need a single chamber that takes a whole tray. Each basket here is 3 Qt.
  • You have no width to spare: 13.38 in across, 13.3 lb to lift onto the shelf afterwards.
