How much counter space does an air fryer actually need? Measure this first

Every model publishes a footprint, a weight and an electrical figure. Here is the ten-minute measurement to run before you choose one.

The short answer

Every model publishes a footprint and a weight. Measure the cabinet clearance, the depth of the counter and the distance to the outlet before you choose a size — those three numbers decide more purchases than any feature list does.

The ten-minute measurement

  1. 1
    Width and depth of the free counter. Measure the space the appliance will actually occupy, not the run of counter. Add the width of whatever else stays beside it.
  2. 2
    Height under the cabinet. These appliances are 10.6 in, 13.5 in and 14.8 in tall as published. If a cabinet is lower than the appliance plus its ventilation clearance, it will not go under it.
  3. 3
    Distance to the outlet. All three publish 120 V operation, and two of them publish 1700 W. Keep the appliance where you can plug it in directly rather than reaching for an extension lead.
  4. 4
    Room above the door. The 26-quart model opens two French doors outward; the space in front of the counter has to allow both of them to open.

What this article deliberately does not tell you

How long it takes to cook anything, how much it costs to run, and what it looks like in a real kitchen. Those are measured or observed facts, and no official page states them, so they are not on this site. The Flagged for Review page lists every figure this project could not confirm.
