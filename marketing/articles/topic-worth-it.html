Where the trend outruns the evidence

  • "450 °F Hi-Fry" is model-specific. The Chefman 8 Qt TurboFry's Amazon title advertises 450 °F, but its own spec block and Chefman's manufacturer page say 200–400 °F for the 8 Qt model. The 450 °F figure belongs to the 6 Qt dual-window model. Published as a conflict (Flag F-22) — do not buy the 8 Qt expecting 450 °F.
  • Preset counts disagree even on one manufacturer page. The Emeril 26QT's description says "24 one-touch cooking presets," its spec tile says "25 Cooking Pre-sets," and a feature block lists ten functions. Published as a conflict (Flag F-19) — the number is not settled.
  • Model numbers disagree between Amazon and the manufacturer. Amazon lists Emeril 26QT as 4-00675-02X-VN; Emeril's own store says 4-00675-02A-VN. Both published side by side (Flag F-23).

Who each model actually fits

  • Chefman 6 Qt dual basket — two independent baskets, Sync Finish so both finish together, dual viewing windows. Good for cooking two things at once (e.g. fries and nuggets) for 1–3 people.
  • Emeril Lagasse 26QT French Door — the largest of the three, dual French doors, rotisserie, 10-in-1 modes. Fits a whole chicken or 12-inch pizza; suits 4–6 people.
  • Chefman 8 Qt TurboFry — single stainless-steel basket, 4 presets, LED shake reminder, dishwasher-safe basket. A straightforward 4–6 person air fryer.
