How these articles are written

  • Every claim has a source. Each section ends with the official page it was read from, and every row of every table carries the source of that row.
  • Nothing is inferred. Where no official source states a value, the article says so instead of filling the gap — including the questions the guidance does not answer.
  • Conflicts stay visible. Where a manufacturer's page and an Amazon listing disagree, both are shown and neither is presented as the answer.
  • No prices, no ratings. The program's operating rules restrict how prices may be displayed, and ratings are storefront-specific and volatile. Neither is printed here.
  • Nothing here is hands-on testing. No member of this project has cooked with these appliances. Performance claims are the manufacturers' and are attributed to them.

A full description of the method is on the verification page, and every open question is on the flagged-for-review page.
