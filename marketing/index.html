Three air fryers, one honest spec sheet

A buying guide, a side-by-side comparison and one article per product — every number traced to the manufacturer's or Amazon's own page.

Most product write-ups ask you to trust the adjectives. These pages do the opposite: where the manufacturer's page and Amazon's listing agree, you see the number; where they disagree — and for one model they do — you see both statements and decide for yourself; and where nobody official states a fact, you see the blank instead of a confident guess. That discipline is unusual on an affiliate site, and it is deliberate: an appliance recommendation you can check is worth more than one you can't.

Start with the buying guide if you're choosing between them, or jump straight to a product article below if you already know the model you're researching.
