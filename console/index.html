Executive summary

The products are fine. The site is the problem, and the problem is that it describes itself. The published tree holds 214 pages across 18 namespaces — 222 counting this console — every value traced to an official source, 13,590 internal links with 0 broken, and the mandatory disclosure on 214 of 214 pages. The verification work is genuinely done and genuinely good. These counts are measured at build time, not typed: five other sessions merged into this tree while this review ran, and a typed number went stale twice in an afternoon.

Until today it was invisible to a reader. The public landing page opened with the client's own requirement list and a strip of figures about the project's audit state rather than with the three air fryers. A redesign by another session (PR #108) merged while this review was running and removed both, which is the same conclusion reached here independently. Two traces remain: the page still heads itself AI-powered Amazon affiliate platform, and it still says the three listings are identified when no official document binds the supplied codes to them.

Revenue is blocked by one missing identifier, not by a technical gap. Every Amazon link must carry the assigned Associates ID as a URL parameter; none of the three supplied links does, and no Associates ID exists in the repository. The three supplied codes are 9 characters where Amazon defines the ASIN as “10-digit”, and a control run today confirms the short-link service refuses even a valid ASIN, so the codes are opaque identifiers rather than truncated product numbers. What they resolve to is reproducible and recorded; what the brief intended them to mean is not, and no official document settles it.

Two things need permission before they can be built, not after. Program Content may not be used to develop machine-learning models without Amazon's express prior written approval — which is precisely what the brief asks for. And catalog data (prices, ratings) is lawful only through the Creators API, which needs enrolment plus ten qualifying sales in thirty days. Both are gated on the same missing account.

What can be decided today, with nothing else needed: the editorial items. Six question-shaped articles are writable right now from rows the project already holds and already sources. They need no Associates account, no API, and no new data. They are also the only items on this board that would move the site toward Amazon's own stated standard — a site that gives a reader “insight on a subject or product they might not get easily”.
