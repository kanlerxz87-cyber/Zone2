OUR APPROACH

Less guesswork. More useful questions.

Counter Notes is an independent, AI-assisted kitchen buying publication. We help you compare the information that matters before buying; we do not pretend a spec sheet is a hands-on test.

Who makes Counter Notes?

The publication is maintained through this repository and AI-assisted research and development. “Counter Notes” is the organizational author, not an invented person. No named human editor, professional testing credential, or physical product testing is claimed. A designated human review and contact process is an open item on the questions register.

Our guides combine source information with explicitly editorial decision exercises. We do not reproduce customer reviews, star ratings or claimed personal experience. Federal Trade Commission · FTC ↗ Google Search Central · GOOGLE-HELPFUL ↗

How we use sources

Product rows link to manufacturer pages or official Amazon listings and carry a checked date. A manufacturer statement is attributed as a statement, not treated as our own test result. An unresolved conflict stays unresolved; a value outside our verification scope stays blank.

The complete historical research remains accessible from the Workspace. New source checks on 19 November 2026 are scoped to the current publication’s rows and guides—not a claim that every historical sentence was re-verified. Read the dated verification record.

How our affiliate links work

As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases. Product buttons marked “paid link” point directly to Amazon using the owner-supplied tracking ID shopnow047c-20. Amazon handles pricing, checkout and orders.

A correctly shaped link is not proof of enrollment or attribution. We have not accessed an authenticated Associates tracking report. The supplied short links’ exact compliance status remains unresolved; we do not call them invalid. Intended product identity and observed redirect destinations are kept in different fields in the intent register. Amazon Associates · AMZ-AGREEMENT ↗ Amazon Associates · AMZ-LINKS ↗

Why there are no prices, ratings or “best” scores

No licensed, fresh API pricing or ratings feed is connected. We therefore do not display current prices, availability, customer star ratings or review excerpts. Price and review-data automation stays gated on authorized access and policy checks. No popularity, conversion or revenue forecast is offered. What the images show

The front-page food image is AI-generated editorial artwork, not a meal cooked in any featured appliance. The original appliance and tool illustrations show generic formats, not exact product appearance, scale or dimensions. They are labelled accordingly. No Amazon product photography is copied or cached.

Corrections, privacy and new guides

Known uncertainties are published on Flagged for Review. Review choices live on the suggestions board. Read the privacy notice and follow our RSS feed for new guides. Email capture is not presented as working without a real subscription service.
