5. Anti-“AI slop” editorial rules (paste into the content prompt)

  1. Source gate: no sentence prints a value without a link to the official page it came from; unsourceable cells render “unverified” with the reason — never an inferred value.
  2. No invented experience: never write “we tested”, “in our kitchen” or first-person results unless the owner actually performed the test (SG-05 undecided).
  3. No banned surfaces: no prices (C-03), no star ratings/review counts (C-07), no price-drop or price-alert widgets (C-08).
  4. Specificity / swap-the-model test: if a paragraph survives swapping in a different model name, delete it — that is filler (C-13’s “insight … not available anywhere else”).
  5. Scale guard: publish no page whose main purpose is covering a keyword; every page must help a user do something (C-16 verbatim).
  6. Thin-affiliation check before publish: does the piece add at least one of Google’s named value-adds — extra information, original review, rigorous testing we actually did, navigation, comparison? If none: do not publish (C-17 verbatim).
  7. Named authorship & editorial responsibility on marketing pages (Google’s site-reputation guidance treats stated authorship and editorial integration as review factors — observed 2026-11-15).
  8. Disclosure placement: the disclosure sentence in the first screenful and next to link blocks (C-01, C-18).
  9. Freshness dates visible; re-verify spec rows at least annually and after any destination change; remove expired promotion mentions the day they end (C-06).
  10. Human editorial pass: generation may draft, but a pass applying rules 1–9 is mandatory before publish — record who approved it (mirrors Google’s “editorial oversight” factor).
