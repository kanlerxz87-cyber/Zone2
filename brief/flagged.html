FB-06
open high
 Zero of 138 published pages carry an Associates tag, so no page on the site can currently earn

Measured with Amazon's published tag format (NAME-NN). The three supplied short links resolve without a tag parameter. Amazon's own application-review page states that the tags 'must be embedded in your Amazon product links on the site(s) declared in your application', and that the application is checked once at least three qualifying sales have been driven within the first 180 days.

Why it matters. This is the finding the brief's 'so real people can click the links and buy' runs into. Until a tag exists, traffic cannot convert into qualifying purchases, and without three of them the application is never reviewed. Everything downstream — conversion data, the optimizer, the revenue model — is blocked behind one string the owner has to supply.
