Choose a path · 2026-11-16

Twelve proposals. Nothing implemented until you pick one.

The rows are listed in the order that unblocks the most. DB-06 and DB-01 are prerequisites for almost everything else: one declares what the site is, the other removes the label that makes it read as generated from the pages T55 did not rebuild.

How to record a choice

Open brief/data/decisions.json, set the row's 'choice' to the letter of the option you want, add a note, and set decided_by and decided_on. The next build re-renders the board with the choice shown on the row. Or tell the agent the IDs and it will record them in the same form.

Costs and risks are the reviewer's estimates, deliberately coarse, and are labelled as estimates — they are not program facts. Rows below marked blocked on a question cannot be decided until the named question on the information register is answered.

DB-01 · Stop describing the site as AI-powered on the operator and companion pages

Public copy awaiting a choice sequence 2 — the reader-facing half is already done by T55

What changes

Remove 'AI-powered' and 'AI-powered Amazon affiliate' from the 14 pages that still print them: console/, research-hub/, summary.html, review-trace.html, affiliate-lab/, zone2/. The T55 rebuild already cleared every reader-facing page on 2026-11-16.

Why

It is the brief's vocabulary surviving into the deliverable, and it is the strongest single signal that the copy was produced from a commission rather than from the work. Google's guidance names the risk it invites (BR-13); Amazon's content bar asks for robust original content (BR-08). No reader has ever chosen an affiliate site because it was AI-powered.

Options

  • A. Remove from every public page; keep one note on the owner console
    Reader-facing copy loses the claim entirely; the provenance is still recorded where only the owner looks.
  • B. Remove everywhere, including owner surfaces
    Cleanest, but loses the record of how the pages were produced.
  • C. Keep, and add a sentence explaining what is and is not automated
    Honest, but puts the word back in front of the reader, which is the problem.
RecommendedA
Effortestimate: under an hour; a find-and-replace plus a read of each affected page
Riskestimate: none to compliance. A cosmetic risk that some pages read plainer.
Reversibleyes

DB-04 · Move the disclosure into the recommendation block instead of the page footer

Compliance as design awaiting a choice sequence 4

What changes

Every product recommendation renders inside a bordered block that carries the §5 sentence above the verdict, not in a strip at the top or a line at the bottom.

Why

Both governing sources ask for proximity to the endorsement, not presence on the page: the Operating Agreement says 'clearly and prominently' (BR-05), and the FTC says the disclosure 'should be placed with the endorsement message itself' and is 'likely to be missed' at the end of a post (BR-17). The site currently satisfies the letter and misses the point.

Options

  • A. Disclosure inside every recommendation block
    Repeats the sentence on pages with several recommendations, which is what the FTC wants.
  • B. Disclosure at the top of the page and inside the first recommendation only
    Less repetition; weaker on the second and later recommendations.
RecommendedA
Effortestimate: half a day across the marketing templates
Riskestimate: low, and it reduces compliance risk rather than adding it
Reversibleyes

DB-05 · Make the blank cell a designed state rather than an absence

Presentation awaiting a choice sequence 5

What changes

An empty cell renders as an en-dash on a dotted rule, with a tooltip and a visible legend naming which rule or absence makes it empty. Three semantic states across the whole site: sourced (ink), blank (muted), conflict (amber, both values shown).

Why

The site's discipline is its differentiator, and it currently renders as a gap. Google's own description of a good affiliate page is a page that adds value (BR-12); a blank with a stated reason is added value, a bare dash is not.

Options

  • A. Full three-state treatment with a legend on every table
    Best signal; a legend on every table costs a little vertical space.
  • B. Tooltip only, no visible legend
    Tighter, but the reason is invisible on touch devices.
RecommendedA
Effortestimate: a day across the table components
Riskestimate: low

DB-06 · Declare one site, and demote the other thirteen to linked evidence

Site architecture awaiting a choice sequence 1 — it determines what DB-02 and DB-03 are rewriting

What changes

Pick the reader-facing surface as the declared Site. Every other namespace stays published but becomes reachable only from it. Sixteen surfaces publish today — two more than before T55 and this namespace landed.

Why

Google's people-first guidance names as a warning sign content 'spread across a large network of sites, so that individual pages or sites don't get as much attention or care' (BR-15). This repository publishes 14 surfaces (RV-16). Amazon's application asks for the Site list to be kept current and to name exact URLs (BR-10's page), and RR-11 on the readiness board is already a GAP for exactly this reason.

Options

  • A. Counter Notes (marketing/) is the site; everything else is evidence behind it
    Cleanest for an application, and the only surface already written for readers. Costs a navigation rebuild.
  • B. Keep the root dashboard as the site and fold the editorial into it
    Preserves the work already done on the dashboard, but the dashboard is the surface that reads as generated.
  • C. Leave fourteen surfaces and declare the root URL only
    No work, but the owner is submitting a site whose own architecture contradicts the content bar.
RecommendedA
Effortestimate: several days
Riskestimate: medium — it is the largest change on this board, and it touches navigation that five namespaces depend on

DB-07 · Publish a named byline with a true one-line scope statement

Trust awaiting a choice sequence 6

What changes

Every reader-facing article carries an author and a sentence saying what that person actually did — for example, that they read the three listings and the manufacturer pages on a stated date. Never 'tested'.

Why

Google asks whether content demonstrates first-hand expertise and whether there is background about the author (BR-16). The FTC is blunter: you cannot talk about experience with a product you have not tried (BR-18). A byline that states the real scope is both the honest answer and the strongest trust signal available without a testing programme.

Options

  • A. Real name, true scope sentence, dated
    Best for trust; requires the owner to be named publicly.
  • B. Desk name (Counter Notes) with a true scope sentence
    No personal exposure; weaker on the expertise signal Google describes.
  • C. No byline until a testing programme exists
    Safest, and leaves the trust signal on the table indefinitely.
RecommendedA
B if the owner prefers not to be named publicly.
Effortestimate: an hour plus the decision
Riskestimate: low in itself; the decision of whether to be named is the owner's

DB-08 · Set body text at 18–19px on a 62–70 character measure

Typography awaiting a choice sequence 7

What changes

Body size up from the browser default, measure down from the current wide grid, serif display headlines, humanist sans for body. Cards and trust strips move below the fold.

Small text on a long measure is the look of a rendered table, and it is the second-largest contributor to the generated feel after the vocabulary. This is a craft judgement, not a rule; no official source states a font size.

DB-09 · Set a re-verification cadence, or take the dates off the pages

Evidence maintenance awaiting a choice sequence 8

What changes

Either commit to re-fetching the load-bearing official pages on a stated interval and reprinting the date, or stop printing verification dates altogether.

Why

Every page on the site prints a verification date that is already between one and three days old, and the oldest date any page prints is 2026-11-13. Google warns against changing dates to make content seem fresh when it has not substantively changed. A date that no one maintains is worse than no date: it is a claim that decays silently.

Options

  • A. Quarterly re-verification, date printed and enforced by a gate
    Highest integrity, and a recurring cost.
  • B. Re-verify only when a page is edited
    Cheap; the date stops meaning 'checked' and starts meaning 'written'.
  • C. Remove printed dates
    Honest and free, and loses a genuine differentiator.
RecommendedA
C if the site is going to be parked rather than pursued.
Effortestimate: an hour to set the interval; a day to build the gate that enforces it
Riskestimate: low
Reversibleyes

DB-11 · Instrument the site before writing another article

Measurement blocked on a question sequence blocked — cannot start until the tag exists (BQ-14) and the account is approved (BQ-19)

What changes

Once a tag exists, add click-level measurement of the outbound product links and a dated record of what was clicked. Until then, write nothing new.

Why

Every other proposal on this board is a judgement about what readers want, and today there is not one measured reader. Amazon's page states plainly that it makes no representation about the traffic or commission income any associate can expect (BR-06); the only way to replace that with knowledge is to measure this site.

Options

  • A. Instrument first, then write
    Slower to publish; every later editorial decision has evidence behind it.
  • B. Write the ten posts Amazon's content bar asks for, then instrument
    Clears the application bar sooner; the second ten articles get written blind too.
