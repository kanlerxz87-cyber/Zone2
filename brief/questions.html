What an official page settles, what nothing can, and what we already hold

Three registers. The first quotes only what a linked official page says. The second is empty by design: an open question's answer cell is never filled with an inference. The third is what the project already has, and how each item was established.

Answered — 13 questions an official page settles

QuestionAnswer, as the page states itSource
May the site show Amazon star ratings and review counts?Not as configured. The Program Policies allow them only when obtained through Amazon's API: “You will not display or otherwise use any of our customer reviews or star ratings, in part or in whole, on your Site unless you have obtained a link to that customer review or star rating through Creators API or PA API and you comply with the requirements for Creators API and PA API described in the License.”BR-01
0 of 138 published pages show one (RV-04's sibling measurement; the readiness board's own figure is 0).
Must the product links on the declared site carry an Associates tag?Yes. “Associates special links are the identifiers (e.g. abcd-20) embedded in Amazon product links. These tags must be embedded in your Amazon product links on the site(s) declared in your application.” The same page states that an account rejected for missing tagged links is told to correct them.BR-10
0 of 138 published pages carry a tag in that format (RV-04).
How many sales must the site drive before the application is reviewed?“After you sign up, our Associates team will check your application once you’ve driven qualified sales (we require at least three within the first 180 days).” The same page adds that personal orders do not qualify.BR-07
0. No tagged link exists, so no qualifying purchase can be attributed (RV-04).
What content bar must the declared site clear?“All Sites must have robust original content (even when advertising is removed) – a good rule of thumb is at least 10 posts. They must be publicly available (for example, they should not be a closed group, or behind a paywall).” Rejection for unoriginal or insufficient content is final: “we aren't able to reassess an application once it's been rejected.”BR-08
14 candidate editorial posts are counted, clearing the rule of thumb; the readiness board still marks the row GAP because every counted page is a specification or comparison surface about these three listings (RV-15).
How recent must the content be, and who must own the site?“Content on your website must be recent (generally within the last 60 days). You must own your website.”BR-09
The oldest date any published page prints for its own content is 2026-11-13, three days before this review. Ownership is not established by anything in the repository.
Does Amazon promise the site any traffic or income if it does the work?No. “We do not make any representation, warranty, or covenant regarding the amount of traffic or commission income you can expect at any time in connection with the Associates Program, and we will not be liable for any actions you undertake based on your expectations.”BR-06
Consistent with the site publishing no performance figures anywhere.
May the site say the products were tested?No. The FTC's guidance is flat: “You can’t talk about your experience with a product you haven’t tried.” Google asks whether the content “clearly demonstrate[s] first-hand expertise and a depth of knowledge (for example, expertise that comes from having actually used a product or service, or visiting a place)”.BR-18 · BR-16
No page claims testing. The byline question is open (BQ-16).
Where must the affiliate disclosure sit on a page?With the recommendation, not at the end. The FTC: “Place it so it’s hard to miss. The disclosure should be placed with the endorsement message itself”, and warns that disclosures “are likely to be missed if they appear only on an ABOUT ME or profile page, at the end of posts or videos, or anywhere that requires a person to click MORE.” Amazon's agreement requires the statement to be stated “clearly and prominently”.BR-17 · BR-05
138 of 138 pages carry the exact sentence, but on the reader-facing pages it sits in a strip above the content rather than inside the recommendation (RV-03, DB-04).
May the site offer a reader anything for clicking through?No. “You will not offer any person or entity any consideration, reward, or incentive (including any money, rebate, discount, points, donation to charity or other organization, or other benefit) for using Special Links.”BR-03
No page offers one.
May the site use short links that hide the Amazon destination?Not if the destination is unclear. “You will not use a link shortening service, button, hyperlink or other ad placement in a manner that makes it unclear that you are linking to an Amazon Site”, and separately you may not “cloak, hide, spoof, or otherwise obscure the URL of your Site containing Special Links”.BR-04
The three links this whole project rests on are themselves short links on link.amazon, a domain the canonical register flags as not the documented public Associates short-link domain (F-04). Whether §6(w) applies to them is not settled by any page this session could read.
What does Google say about writing about trending topics?It is listed as a warning sign: “Are you writing about things simply because they seem trending and not because you'd write about them otherwise for your existing audience?” The same list asks “Are you producing lots of content on many different topics in hopes that some of it might perform well in search results?”BR-14
This is the rule the topics board on this site is built to satisfy.
Is AI-generated content allowed?Google's answer is about scale and value, not about the tool: “using generative AI tools or other similar tools to generate many pages without adding value for users may violate Google's spam policy on scaled content abuse”. The spam policy defines the abuse as “when many pages are generated for the primary purpose of manipulating search rankings and not helping users”.BR-13 · BR-11
No page on this site is generated at request time; all are static files built from JSON ledgers. Twelve pages describe the site as AI-powered anyway (RV-09).
Can an affiliate site be good, by Google's own standard?Yes, and it says how: “Not every site that participates in an affiliate program is a thin affiliate. Good affiliate sites add value by offering meaningful content or features. Examples of good affiliate pages include offering additional information about price, original product reviews, rigorous testing and ratings, navigation of products or categories, and product comparisons.”BR-12
Of the five named value-adds, this site can currently claim two: product comparisons and category navigation. Price information, original reviews and rigorous testing ratings are all closed to it by BQ-01 and BQ-07.
