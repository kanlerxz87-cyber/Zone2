Two 3-quart baskets or one 6-quart basket: the meal maths, not the marketing
comparison proposal BR-19
 The dual-basket pitch is sold as capacity. It is actually a scheduling feature: two baskets let two foods finish together, and the number that decides whether that matters is the capacity of each basket, not the total. Work the arithmetic on the published figures and say when the split helps and when it is a smaller oven in disguise. The dual-basket model's published capacity, and the separate per-basket capacity, as published with sources on the comparison table. judgement: 'two basket air fryer' is a decision query with a real fork in it, and almost every page answering it is a listicle. A page that does the arithmetic is the kind of page that gets linked.
Missing: Nothing structural. Needs the per-basket figure re-verified on the manufacturer page, which is carried rather than re-fetched (BR-22).
Risk: Low. Must not imply the machines were cooked in.
Will a 26-quart air fryer oven fit under your cabinets? The clearance nobody publishes
transactional proposal BR-20
 Every site lists the footprint. Nobody lists the cabinet clearance, the door swing, or the depth you lose to the handle. Take the published height and depth, state the geometry problem explicitly, and give the reader the two measurements to take at home. Where no official page states a clearance figure, print the blank — and say that the blank is the most useful thing on the page. The published dimensions of the 26-quart model, with its source. judgement: 'will it fit' is the question that precedes a return, not a purchase, and it is answered almost nowhere with real numbers.
Missing: Cabinet clearance is not published by anyone. The article's honesty about that is its angle, not a weakness.
Risk: Low, and it turns a compliance constraint into the article's whole premise.
Three air fryers, three temperature ranges: what the spread between 200 and 500 degrees actually buys you
informational proposal BR-19 · BR-21
 The three machines on this site top out at different temperatures. Most coverage treats a higher maximum as strictly better. It is not: above roughly 400 degrees the difference is browning versus burning, and the useful comparison is the range you will actually cook in, not the ceiling. The three published temperature ranges, each with its source; the known conflict where two official pages disagree about one model's maximum (carried as F-22). judgement: 'air fryer temperature' queries are high-intent and almost always answered with a single number rather than a range comparison.
Missing: The conflict has to be shown, not resolved. That is the point.
Risk: Medium — the two-official-pages conflict must be presented as a conflict, and the article must not pick a winner.
What a French door on an air fryer oven changes — and what it costs you in counter space
comparison proposal BR-20
 The door is the decision, not a feature. A drop-down door with two handles needs swing room; a sliding basket needs vertical room. Say which counter each machine suits, using the published dimensions rather than a lifestyle photo. The 26-quart model's published dimensions and door configuration. judgement: shoulders the 'air fryer oven vs basket' query with a geometric answer instead of a feature list, which is what that query currently returns.
Missing: Swing clearance is not published. Say so.
Risk: Low.
24 presets, 4 presets, and the ones people actually use: reading a control panel like a spec sheet
informational proposal BR-20 · BR-21
 Preset counts are marketed as capability. Read the actual preset lists side by side and ask the only useful question: how many of these would a person press twice? The honest answer for most panels is three. The published preset lists for the three models; the manufacturer and listing pages disagree on one model's count, which is already carried as a conflict. judgement: low competition, high specificity, and it is the rare spec page that is genuinely useful before a purchase.
Missing: The preset lists have to be re-read from the manufacturer pages (carried today, BR-22).
Risk: Medium — the disagreement between pages must be shown, not averaged.
Dishwasher-safe, non-stick, and the maintenance spec that decides whether you keep using it
informational proposal BR-19 · BR-21

The published dishwasher-safe and basket-material statements for each model. judgement: 'are air fryer baskets dishwasher safe' is a real query with thin, low-quality answers, and it is answerable entirely from published text.

Air fryer or convection oven: what three spec sheets can settle, and what they cannot
informational proposal BR-16 · BR-12
 The query is asked as if there were a verdict. There is not one in these documents. Settle the part that can be settled — cavity size, wattage, temperature range — and state plainly that the part people actually care about, whether the food is better, is not something a specification can answer. The published capacity, wattage and temperature figures for all three. judgement: this is the highest-volume query in the category and the one where honesty is most differentiating, because the competing pages all fake a verdict.
Missing: Anything about cooking results. The article says so.
Risk: Medium — it must refuse the verdict the query is asking for, which costs some traffic and buys the site its credibility.
