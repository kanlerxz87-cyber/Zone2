Content rules enforced by the platform

The generator refuses an entry it cannot source. These rules run before any article is rendered:

  • Product claims are limited to facts verified on an official Amazon or manufacturer page, with a source link beside each value — no invented features, prices or ratings.
  • Comparisons link each product to its official Amazon page; no cross-retailer price comparison is published without serving prices per program policy.
  • The mandatory Associates disclosure appears on every page and article, and outbound product links carry rel="sponsored" per Google’s paid-link guidance.
  • Limited-time promotion mentions are removed as soon as the promotion ends on Amazon.
