What we won't do

No invented conversion rates. No "estimated monthly revenue" charts built from nothing. No demand curves traced from vibes. Amazon itself says it makes no representation about the traffic or commission income anyone can expect (Operating Agreement §4) — a site that pretends otherwise would be selling a fantasy. When real click and earnings reports exist, this page gets a real performance section; until then it stays honest and short.
