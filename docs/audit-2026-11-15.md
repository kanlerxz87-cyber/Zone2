## 3. What the review concludes

The site's central claim holds. Of 705 table rows, every one either ends in an official-domain
source link or is an explicit blank; the 18 exceptions are internal contract rows (template paths,
linter error codes, register counts), and they are listed rather than waved through. No invented
price, rating, review count or revenue figure was found anywhere. The disclosure sentence required
by the Operating Agreement is on every page.

The site still cannot earn, and mostly for reasons that are not technical:

1. **No measurement exists** (A-05). Zero analytics, zero click endpoint, zero Associates report.
   The optimization and tracking pages describe a capability that has never processed a click.
2. **No tagged link exists** (carried flag F-07/F-24). No page in the repository carries an
   Associates `tag` parameter; the three supplied `link.amazon` URLs are untagged by their own
   nature. Confirmed again this session for two of the three.
3. **No trust surface exists** (A-07). No author, no About page, no contact route, no date — the two
   questions Google's own guidance asks first are unanswered on every one of 116 pages.
4. **No reader can see the tables without JavaScript** (A-01), which is the page a visitor lands on.
5. **The site publishes seven parallel descriptions of three products** (A-06), which is the shape
   Google's scaled-content section describes and the opposite of what a test desk would publish.

## 7. Limits, stated rather than implied

- **No browser.** The JavaScript-rendered tables were measured as *absent from the HTML*, not as
  broken at runtime. A-01 claims exactly what was measured.
- **No credentials.** No Associates account, Creators API key or Search Console property was
  available; every row that needs one is a gap (G-01…G-15), not a guess.
- **No re-transcription of product data.** Two of the three supplied links were re-fetched and their
  destinations confirmed; no price, rating or review count was copied from them, because the site is
  not permitted to display those without an API source.
- **One reader.** A second reader would find more. The findings are published so the next session
  starts from them rather than re-deriving them.
