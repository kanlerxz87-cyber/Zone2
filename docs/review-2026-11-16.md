## Open irregularities and decision prerequisites

These are non-blocking for the site build and are already represented in the published review registers. No value was inferred to close them.

1. **Intended product identity remains unconfirmed.** The source tokens are nine characters and their current redirects only establish observed destinations. Obtain each full Amazon product URL/ASIN from the account owner and confirm against the Associates account/listing before treating these as the intended products. See [F-01/F-03](https://kanlerxz87-cyber.github.io/Zone2/flagged.html).
2. **Affiliate links are not monetized as supplied.** The supplied destinations do not include an Associates ID/tag. The account owner must provide/use a valid Associates tag and regenerate the compliant Special Links. The review environment cannot access Associates Central. See [F-24](https://kanlerxz87-cyber.github.io/Zone2/flagged.html).
3. **Amazon account/API readiness is unknown.** Approval state, Associate ID, target marketplace, and Creators API eligibility are account-specific. No account was connected to this session; these remain unavailable, not assumed false or true.
4. **No live performance or trend evidence is connected.** The published measurement register defines events, but has **0 live events**. Consequently clicks, conversions, commission revenue, trending status, high-conversion claims, competition scores, and traffic forecasts remain blank or explicitly unmeasured. The site does not claim organic traffic or sales outcomes.
5. **Editorial should not imply hands-on testing.** The FTC source says an endorser cannot talk about experience with an untried product. Current content is framed from cited specifications and avoids claiming first-hand product testing. Any future review/testing language requires actual evidence and a documented method.
6. **Companion canonical metadata.** The metadata audit reports 44 companion pages without self-referencing canonicals. The canonical contract records these as report-only under an existing owner decision. If those companion namespaces are intended for organic search, choose one public canonical site and then either consolidate or provide a documented canonical strategy before expanding them.
