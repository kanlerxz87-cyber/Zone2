  • 4Record the decision and move the status. Leaving a proposal at PROPOSED is how a register turns into a wish list.
  • 5Questions that block a decision live in the Questions register, with the exact step that closes each one.

SG-01 · Identity

Lock product identity before writing anything else about the products

PROPOSED

The problem it addresses

The three supplied codes are 9 characters; Amazon's own help page defines an ASIN as the 10-character code for a specific product. The three link.amazon URLs resolve reproducibly to one specific destination each, and an unknown code returns a 404 - so the tokens are honoured - but no public Amazon document binds a code to a product, and the destination token rotates between days. Every product row on the site is therefore published as 'the product this link resolves to' rather than 'the product the brief named'.

Evidence: S24 · affiliate-program.amazon.comofficial S19 · www.amazon.comofficial S20 · www.amazon.comofficial S21 · www.amazon.comofficial S32 · link.amazoncontrol S33 · link.amazonsupplied S34 · link.amazonsupplied S35 · link.amazonsupplied

Options and their trade-offs

Every option is recorded with what it gains and what it costs.
OptionAdvantagesCosts
A. Owner confirms the three destinations against the brief, or supplies the 10-character ASINsUnblocks every identity-dependent decision at once; the mapping then rests on a stated fact rather than on observed redirect behaviour.Depends on the brief owner responding; nothing can proceed in the meantime.
B. Treat the resolved destinations as the products permanentlyNo owner dependency; the site already publishes this position and every row is already sourced.If the brief meant different products, every article, comparison and keyword was chosen for the wrong items - and the error is invisible until someone notices.
C. Keep both readings side by side and label each product page with the uncertaintyFully transparent; a reader can judge the risk.Weakens every page commercially and confuses search engines with duplicate intent; it is the current position and it is already published as a flag.

Recommendation

Option A, with C kept as the published position until it is answered. Option B is the worst of the three once the campaign registry and structured data are switched on, because it silently hard-codes an unverified identity into the automation.

Expected effect

Product pages, campaigns, structured data and commission modelling stop carrying an identity caveat; the 'product identity is not confirmed' banner can be removed from every published surface.

Effort

Estimate: one message to the link provider; the rest of the work is already scaffolded (tools/asin_guard.py, tools/amazon_creators_api.py).

Risk

Low to implement. The risk of NOT doing it is the material one: publishing confidently about the wrong product is exactly the misleading-claim failure the Participation Requirements forbid.

SG-02 · Monetisation

Configure the Associates ID and regenerate every outbound link as a Special Link

PROPOSED

The problem it addresses

Amazon's help page gives the qualifying link format as a tagged /dp/ link, and the Participation Requirements state that a Special Link must use the assigned Associates ID and be accessed directly from the Site. Every URL the three supplied links resolve to carries no tag parameter, so as supplied they cannot attribute a sale. The repository stores no ID and may not invent one; the link builder therefore runs in inert preview mode and every product link on the site is labelled as earning nothing.

Evidence: S02 · affiliate-program.amazon.comofficial S24 · affiliate-program.amazon.comofficial S19 · www.amazon.comofficial S20 · www.amazon.comofficial S21 · www.amazon.comofficial

Every option is recorded with what it gains and what it costs.
OptionAdvantagesCosts

B. Use Amazon's own link-creation tooling (SiteStripe / Product Links) instead of building URLsAmazon generates the tagged link, so the format is correct by construction; no credential is stored in the repository.Manual per-link work; campaign keys and first-party click counting then live outside the repository.
C. Keep the preview links and publish the site as a non-earning research projectNo account needed; nothing to mis-configure.The site cannot earn; the disclosure sentence would be shown on a site with no affiliate links at all, which reads as misleading in itself.

Option A as the system of record, with option B as the manual fallback while the account is being set up. Option C is only defensible if the site is explicitly labelled a research demo.

Risk

Mis-tagging sends another account's traffic somewhere else; the builder refuses to emit a link whose competing attribution parameters are present, and should be re-checked once a real tag exists.
