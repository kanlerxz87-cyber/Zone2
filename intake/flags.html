Why "does not establish" is its own row

The most common failure in a verification register is a finding that over-reaches by one step: an observation is recorded, then a conclusion is drawn from it that the observation does not support. Every row above separates the two explicitly. The clearest case is IF-02: no visible tag is a measurement; "therefore the links are invalid" would be a conclusion this project refuses to draw, because the brief's own FLG-003 records the compliance status of the supplied short links as unresolved.
