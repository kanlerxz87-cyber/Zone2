Submitted-link intake

Five links were submitted. Three of them were on the site.

The 2026-11-17 brief names five affiliate links and a tracking id. This repository was built around three of them. Two of the codes, and the tracking id, appear nowhere in the tree. This namespace registers all five, and separates what was asked for from what the links measurably do.

The one thing to take away. All five links resolve to a live Amazon product page, reproducibly — but none of the five resolves to a product we can prove was intended, and none of the five URLs we observed carries an Associates tag. Two of the five are not air fryers at all. Those are three separate problems and they have three separate fixes, all of which need one sign-in.

Three findings that change what should be built

1. The submitted set is not one category

Two of the five links (SUB-04, SUB-05) resolve into Home & Kitchen › Kitchen & Dining › Kitchen Utensils & Gadgets › Thermometers & Timers › Meat. Every page on this site that describes the promoted range as air fryers is now wrong about two fifths of it. Recorded as IF-01; the choice is IP-02.

2. The measurement plan cannot be built as specified

The brief asks for a self-hosted analytics endpoint. GitHub's own documentation describes Pages as a static site hosting service, and this site is published on Pages — so there is nowhere on this host to run the thing that would receive the beacons. This is recorded as IF-08 rather than worked around, because the three real options differ in cost and in what they may store, and the choice is IP-04.

3. The compliance question is still open, in both directions

No observed URL carries a tag, which under the Operating Agreement is what a Special Link requires. It does not follow that the supplied links are invalid — a tag may be attached during the redirect in a way this measurement cannot see, which is why the brief's own FLG-003 treats their status as unresolved and why this project refuses to claim either way. Settling it takes one enrolled account and one report read: IP-03.
