AF-06 · Supplied link.amazon/<code> URLs fall back to unrelated pages

Severity: high · Status: open

Each supplied short link resolves to exactly one Amazon product page, and to that same destination on every fetch (re-verified 2026-11-14). The earlier reading — that the plpRedirect=mhFallback marker proved a fallback — did not survive a control test: a bare, untagged fetch of a valid ASIN returns the real product page with the same marker, so the marker is not evidence of a fallback. What remains open is that no public Amazon document binds a link.amazon code to a destination ASIN. link.amazon/ root and /robots.txt returned HTTP 403.

Sources: link.amazon/B04G1MEwY · link.amazon/B02NbDxp3 · link.amazon/B00hJ57DV

AF-07 · Product data cells blank — two different reasons, stated separately

Severity: high (blocks marketing copy) · Status: open

Blank because no official source confirms them: the product name, category, best-seller rank and per-product commission rate. The supplied codes are 9 characters and are not addressable ASINs, so no catalogue record exists for them. This is intentional: the project rule forbids inference.

Blank by policy, though visible on the official pages: price, star rating, review count and availability. Re-fetched 2026-11-14, the destination pages plainly show list prices, star ratings, review counts and stock status. They are withheld because Program Policies §6(a), §6(t) and §6(y) and the IP License require that Program Content to come from Amazon-served links or the Creators API, and §6(t) bars customer reviews and star ratings outright unless obtained through the API. Availability also changes hour to hour, so any published figure would be wrong within a day.

Basis: search returns no results · product page 404 · Program Policies §6(a), §6(t), §6(y)

AF-08 · Per-product commission rate unknown (category unknown)

Severity: medium · Status: open

Amazon's rate card assigns fixed rates by product category. Because P1–P3 categories are unknown, their rates cannot be determined. Program-level rates are published on the Analysis page.

Source: Standard Commission Income rate card

How to clear these flags

  1. Provide valid 10-character ASINs or corrected product URLs → clears AF-01…AF-07 (and AF-08 once category is visible).
  2. Enroll in Amazon Associates and obtain a Tracking ID → clears AF-12 and enables tagged links.
  3. Reach ≥10 qualifying sales in 30 days and register the Creators API → clears AF-10.
  4. Drop any legacy PA-API 5 integration → addresses AF-11.
  5. Sign in to Associates Central to capture the personalized schedule → clears AF-09.
  6. Add the original requirements document to the repo → clears AF-13.
