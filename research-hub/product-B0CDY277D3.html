Who it suits — and who it does not

Read this as a reading of the specifications, not a product test. Nothing on this page comes from handling the appliance. It comes from the sourced numbers above.

Probably a good fit if

  • You want the capacity stated above and you have the counter space for the stated footprint.
  • The cooking modes listed in the feature set match the food you actually cook.
  • You are comfortable relying on the manufacturer's stated warranty terms rather than a retailer's extended plan.

Think twice if

  • You need a capacity the specs do not provide — check the comparison before deciding.
  • Counter space is tight; the dimensions row above is the manufacturer's own figure where one exists.
  • You need to run it on a circuit shared with other high-draw appliances — all three products in this set draw the same high wattage.
