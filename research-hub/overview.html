What this page is, and is not

It is

  • A sourced reference for three Amazon listings: what each product is, who makes it, and what the manufacturer and Amazon each state about it.
  • A record of where those two sources disagree with each other.
  • An honest map of what an affiliate site may and may not publish under Amazon's Associates policies.

It is not

  • A price tracker. No price is printed, because a site may only display Amazon pricing when Amazon serves it or it comes from the official API.
  • A review site. No star rating is printed, because ratings differ by Amazon storefront for the same product.
  • An earnings prediction. The authoritative commission rate card requires an Associates login and could not be read.
