Supplied-link resolution — what each short link actually resolves to

Each submitted link.amazon URL was fetched directly and its final URL recorded, on 2026-11-13 and again on 2026-11-14. A control fetch of a bare, valid ASIN (/dp/B0CDY277D3) returns the same real product page carrying the same plpRedirect=mhFallback and lv=shuf markers — so those markers are not evidence of a fallback. What is verified: the destination ASIN, its title and its category path. What is not verified: that these are the items the brief named, because no public Amazon document binds a link.amazon code to a destination ASIN.
