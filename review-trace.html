Design findings: the “AI slop” complaint, made specific

The complaint in the request is that the site reads like generic generated output. That is a real observation and a useless instruction until it is broken into things that can be seen and counted. Nine findings, each with what a reader actually sees, why it matters, and the proposal that would change it.

Design findings. “Measured by” refers to a row in the measurement table above; “Fix” refers to a row on the review board.

PD-01The most important number on each page is a citation, and the way to buy is a footnote
On a product page the eye is led to a source chip reading 'M4 · www.amazon.com Amazon listing'. There is no button and no statement of what happens if it is clicked; 71 of the 80 product links on the site are styled this way.
Affiliate revenue is a function of qualified clicks. A citation-labelled link is the weakest possible invitation to click, and it is the only invitation on the page.PM-04PS-05S86 · FTCDisclosures 101 for Social Media Influencers (page states November 2019)
