Method &amp; Limits

How each value was checked, what this environment cannot reach, and why some cells are blank on purpose.

Affiliate disclosure: As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases.

What this environment can verify

Page fetches were made with the agent's page-fetch tool. Direct curl from the build sandbox cannot reach any of these hosts: affiliate-program.amazon.com, www.amazon.com, chefman.com and developers.google.com all returned HTTP 000 with exit code 35. Consequence: the live checks in this repository are dated observations with quoted output, not a re-runnable CI step. CI can only re-check internal consistency, which is what the repo's existing gates already do.

The three rules used on every row

  1. Cite or blank. A value appears only if an official source states it; the source is linked in the row.
  2. No inference. A figure is never derived from a sibling listing, a carousel, a search snippet, a similar model, or arithmetic performed here.
  3. Conflicts are shown, not resolved. When two official sources disagree, both are published and neither is presented as definitive.

Why prices, ratings and review counts are withheld

The Associates Program Policies restrict how prices/availability and ratings/reviews may be displayed (they must come from Amazon-served data or the API). Rather than risk a policy breach, no page on this site prints a product price, star rating or review count. Row status is marked Withheld by policy to distinguish a deliberate omission from an unverifiable one. Note RV-09: those two clauses were relied on but not re-read this session, and the conservative choice was retained.
