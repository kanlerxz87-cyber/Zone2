RV-02 · No Associates tag on any resolved destination high

None of the three resolved destination URLs contains a ?tag= Associates ID. Under OA §1 the tagged link format is required for a Special Link, so as supplied these links cannot be relied on to attribute commission.

Disposition: Flagged. Owner should generate tagged links from SiteStripe or the API.

Sources: https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/operating/agreement

RV-03 · The code-to-product mapping is observed behaviour, not documented intent medium

The mapping (B04G1MEwY to B0CDY277D3, B02NbDxp3 to B09B7SB46R, B00hJ57DV to B08DKYBTPH) is reproducible but no public Amazon document binds a short code to a destination ASIN. It is therefore evidence of behaviour rather than proof of intent.

Disposition: Products are presented as 'the product each link resolves to', never as 'the product you selected'. Owner should confirm in Associates Central.

Sources: https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/operating/agreement
