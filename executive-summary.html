3 · Critical finding — identifier irregularity

⚠ The supplied identifiers do not meet the official ASIN format
  • Amazon officially defines an ASIN as "a unique 10-character alphanumeric code assigned to every product listed in the Amazon store" — real examples: B07FZ8S74R, 0451524934. Source: Understanding ASINs — Amazon Selling
  • All three supplied codes measure 9 characters — one short of the required 10.
  • No official source has any record of the codes: Amazon product pages return "Page Not Found"; Google exact-match search for "B04G1MEwY" returns approx. 0 results; the Wayback Machine has no captures; CamelCamelCamel reports not in database.
  • link.amazon/B04G1MEwY redirected to an unrelated product page when fetched (Amazon fallback behavior) — the short link is not a reliable identifier.

Decision (no-inference rule): every product-specific field — title, brand, category, price, rating, review count, BSR, commission rate — is left blank and marked unverified in all site tables. Nothing was inferred, estimated, or filled in. Full detail on the Flagged for Review page.

  • Content — SEO content status; generation paused for unverified products.
  • Compliance — Operating Agreement, Program Policies, Participation Requirements, FTC disclosures with official links.
  • Flagged for Review — irregularities and unverified items with source links.
  • Sources — every source used, for manual review.

5 · Compliance posture

  • The platform design follows the Amazon Associates Operating Agreement and Program Policies (official links on the Compliance page).
  • Mandatory disclosures applied site-wide: "As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases." plus clear, conspicuous, link-level FTC disclosure.
  • No commission income can be earned or tracked until the underlying products are verified with valid, official ASINs and the user's own Associates account is enrolled.

6 · Recommended next action

Re-supply the product identifiers in exactly 10-character ASIN format (e.g., B0XXXXXXXX), taken directly from the product page URL after /dp/, or as shown in the product's Product Information section. Once valid ASINs are provided, product data can be verified line by line from Amazon's official product pages, and the analysis, content, and link-management modules activated.
