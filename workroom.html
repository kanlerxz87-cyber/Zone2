A cadence with a stop button.

Editorial operating plan, not a claim that a scheduled publisher is running.

Every Monday: review authorized query/click reports, verify one existing guide’s sources, then propose at most one substantive update or new question.

Publish only after sources, intent/attribution gates where relevant, disclosure, sponsored links, editorial review and the full build pass. Otherwise hold; do not fill the slot with generated copy.

Monthly: review source drift, broken links, duplicate topics and authorized reported outcomes. Re-check the hosting/privacy controls before enabling new collection.

No topic is labelled trending, high-converting or profitable without dated authorized data. Seasonal packaging is editorial context, not demand evidence. Google · people-first content · Google · Search Console performance.

A useful prompt for the next design iteration.

Design Counter Notes as an independent kitchen publication, not a compliance dashboard. Keep a warm ivory canvas, deep green typography, restrained terracotta accents, generous space and a serif/sans editorial hierarchy. Lead with a product-intent question, an honest shortlist and useful buying guides. Keep the disclosure directly under the masthead and in the footer. Use original/licensed imagery or clearly labelled conceptual illustrations. Never invent a human reviewer, first-hand experience, price, rating, trend or performance score. Keep all research dashboards under one Workroom navigation group. Suggestions need options, risks, missing evidence and a review draft—not an automatic deploy button. Expand only after measured reader queries and authorized reports justify the next topic.

This is a design recommendation, not evidence of market demand. Google · people-first content.
