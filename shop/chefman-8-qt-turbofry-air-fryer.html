Chefman TurboFry Touch 8 Qt Air Fryer

One big basket, four presets, nothing to learn

The straightforward choice: a single 8-quart basket - the largest single basket on this site - four one-touch presets and a shake reminder. Chefman says it is designed for 4-6 people.

A good fit if…

  • You cook one thing in a big batch - a family-size tray of fries or a pile of wings.
  • You want few buttons: four presets (fries, chicken, fish, meat) plus manual time and temperature.
  • You forget to shake the basket: an LED shake reminder prompts you.

Think twice if…

  • Only one basket - two different foods share the same time and temperature.
  • The maximum temperature is published two ways (400 °F by Chefman, '450°F Hi-Fry' in the Amazon title); if high-heat crisping matters to you, confirm it before buying.

Where these specs come from. Specifications come from Chefman's TurboFry Touch 8 Qt page. Its maximum temperature (400 °F) differs from the Amazon title's '450°F Hi-Fry', which may mean the listing describes a different revision; both readings are printed below (flag F-22).
