Chefman TurboFry Touch 6 Qt Dual-Basket Air Fryer

Two 3-quart baskets that finish at the same moment

The one to get if dinner is usually a protein and a side. Two independent baskets, and a Sync Finish setting so both halves of the meal are ready together.

A good fit if…

  • You regularly cook two different things at once - wings in one basket, fries in the other.
  • You want both baskets to finish together without doing the timer math yourself (Sync Finish).
  • You like to watch food brown without pulling a basket: both baskets have viewing windows and there is an interior light.

Think twice if…

  • Each basket is 3 Qt, so one large item has to fit in half of the machine's total capacity.
  • At 13.38 x 11.81 x 10.63 in it is not the smallest unit on this site; measure your counter first.
