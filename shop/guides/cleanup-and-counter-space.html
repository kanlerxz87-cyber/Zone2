Cleanup and counter space

How to assess cleanup and counter-space trade-offs

The appliance you use is the one that is easy to clean and easy to leave out. A practical way to judge both before you buy.

By Counter Notes editors · 2026-11-19 · Figures re-read from official pages that day. We have not tested these products hands-on.

Start with a tape measure

Measure three things: counter depth, the gap under your upper cabinets, and the free width where the appliance would live. Then compare with the published dimensions: the Chefman dual basket is 13.38 x 11.81 x 10.63 in (33.98 x 30 x 27 cm) chefman.com, the Chefman 8 Qt is 15.4 x 12.6 x 13.5 in (39.11 x 32 x 34.29 cm) chefman.com, and the Emeril oven is 16.9 in D x 15.8 in W x 14.8 in H emerileveryday.com.

Leave room to work: basket models need space in front to slide the basket out; door models need the doors to swing.

What actually has to be washed

Look for which parts the maker calls dishwasher-safe. Chefman lists the dual-basket model's baskets as Nonstick, dishwasher-safe chefman.com, and the 8 Qt's as Nonstick, top-rack dishwasher-safe basket and rack chefman.com. An oven-style unit comes with several trays and accessories - more versatility, more pieces to wash and store.

Weight decides whether it moves

If the appliance will live in a cupboard, weight matters every time you use it. Chefman publishes 13.3 lb / 6.03 kg chefman.com for the dual basket and 12.8 lb / 5.8 kg chefman.com for the 8 Qt. We did not find a weight in the specification block of Emeril's store page, so we leave it blank rather than guess.

A simple rule

If you would leave it out, buy for what you cook. If it has to go back in a cupboard after every use, buy the lightest thing that fits your usual meal.

Questions buyers ask

Are air fryer baskets dishwasher safe?

It depends on the model. Chefman lists the baskets on both of its models on this site as dishwasher-safe (the 8 Qt says top-rack). Always check the manufacturer's page for your exact model.
