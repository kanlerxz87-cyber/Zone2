Executive summary

  • What changed today. The front page now leads with the five products instead of the verification process. A new shop section adds one page per product, two category pages (air fryers, meat thermometers) and six question-shaped buying guides. Every dashboard from earlier sessions is still published, reachable from one 'Behind the scenes' menu and the footer.
  • The five links still resolve to the same products. All five supplied link.amazon codes were opened again on 2026-11-19 and landed on the same five listings recorded earlier: Chefman 6 Qt dual basket (B0CDY277D3), Emeril 26 QT French door oven (B09B7SB46R), Chefman 8 Qt (B08DKYBTPH), ThermoMaven instant-read (B0DHZTCNR5), CHEF iQ Sense (B0DZFD6R9Z). None of the resolved URLs carries an Associates tag. link.amazon link.amazon link.amazon link.amazon link.amazon
  • Product buttons carry your tag. Every 'Check today's price' button links to the observed listing with tag shopnow047c-20, is marked rel="sponsored nofollow" and is labelled '(paid link)'. Citation links to official pages are never tagged.
  • The one decision that blocks everything else. Nobody has confirmed that the five observed listings are the products you meant. The site promotes the observed destinations and says so; the intent fields stay blank until you confirm them (questions OQ-01 and OQ-02).
  • The build was quietly broken - now fixed. Since PR #158 the deploy workflow file contained unresolved merge-conflict markers. GitHub rejected it, so every check since then failed with zero jobs, while the local replay script kept reporting 'all green'. The site itself kept deploying through GitHub's branch-based Pages build. Both the file and the replay script's blind spot are fixed (flag F-44).
  • No traffic or sales data exists yet. Nothing has been measured, so no page on this site states a visitor, click, conversion or revenue figure. The click counter counts locally and transmits nothing until an endpoint exists (suggestion SG-S04).
