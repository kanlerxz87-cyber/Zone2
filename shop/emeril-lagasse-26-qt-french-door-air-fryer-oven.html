Oven-style, 10-in-1

Emeril Lagasse French Door AirFryer 360, 26 QT

An oven with French doors that also air fries

Not really a basket air fryer at all: a 26-quart countertop convection oven with dual French doors, a rotisserie and ten cooking functions. It can stand in for a toaster oven too, but it is the deepest machine here.

A good fit if…

  • You want one appliance to roast, bake, broil, dehydrate, proof and air fry.
  • You cook for a group - Emeril sizes it for 4-6 people - or want room for a rotisserie.
  • You prefer opening doors to pulling out a basket.

Think twice if…

  • At 16.9 in deep it needs real counter depth - measure under your upper cabinets.
  • The preset and accessory counts differ between official pages (see the table); go by what matters to you, not by the number.
