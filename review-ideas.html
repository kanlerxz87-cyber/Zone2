What these deliberately are not

Not a trend claim. “Trending” needs a trend series. Until PQ-03 is answered, the honest word is “seasonal” or “question-shaped”, and this board uses those words.

Not decided. Each row says awaiting your decision. Saying yes to a concept here records the choice; writing it is the next task.
