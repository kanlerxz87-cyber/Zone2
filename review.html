How a decision gets recorded

1. You pick a proposal and an option in your reply — for example “PS-05, option A”. Nothing else is needed.

2. The next session sets decision to SELECTED and fills decision_note with your words and the date. The gate fails if a recorded selection names an option the row does not offer, so the record cannot drift from the decision.

3. Only then does an implementation session start — on its own branch, with its own gate — and this board keeps showing the selection rather than erasing it.

A proposal may also be marked DEFERRED, which requires a note saying what would make it decidable. There is no third state: no row disappears.
