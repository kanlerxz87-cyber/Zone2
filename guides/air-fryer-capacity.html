Size without the guesswork

A no-hype checklist for comparing air fryer capacity

Combined quarts are only the starting point. Compare the basket layout and leave unmeasured serving estimates out of it.

By Counter Notes research deskPublished 19 November 2026Source research · not hands-on testing

Separate combined capacity from the space in one basket.

The dual-basket Chefman is described as 6 Qt combined, with two independent 3 Qt baskets in the Amazon title. Chefman’s stainless-steel page separately lists an 8 Qt variant. Those are different layouts, not two ways of describing the same chamber. Chefman · dual-window specifications · Amazon · Chefman dual-basket listing · Chefman · stainless-steel specifications.

The no-hype checklist: Record the total capacity, the number of containers, the exact variant and any stated usable internal dimensions. If the last field is not established by the checked source, leave it blank instead of converting quarts into a guessed portion count.

Do not invent a household-size formula.

This guide does not assign a number of servings to a quart figure. No practical portion test was performed here. Match the container shape to the foods and portions you actually plan to cook, using the manufacturer’s instructions for the exact model.

Check these before adding to basket

  • Is the quoted capacity total, per basket or for a different variant?
  • Does your usual food need one continuous space or separate containers?
  • Can you obtain the usable dimensions if a particular dish must fit?
  • Have you also checked the appliance footprint and required clearance?
