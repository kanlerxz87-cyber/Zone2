The research method

Useful details. Honest limits.

A source-backed buying edit, not a claim that we cooked with or tested these products.

What an official source can tell you

It can establish what a manufacturer or listing says on a particular date. It cannot turn a claimed response time into an independent test, resolve an owner’s product intent, or tell us which product a reader will prefer.

What happens when sources disagree?

We preserve both observations and leave a single normalized value unverified. For example, Chefman’s page states 400°F for its stainless-steel model while the observed Amazon title says 450°F Hi-Fry. Chefman · stainless-steel specifications · Amazon · Chefman 8 Qt listing.

Why no price, stars or “best overall” score?

No licensed live catalog feed, independent product test or authorized conversion report is connected here. This publication chooses not to reproduce static prices or ratings and does not invent a ranking from them. Amazon’s rules place conditions on Program Content, price display and review use. Amazon · Program Policies.

Who wrote this?

AI-assisted source research, not hands-on product testing. No individual author credentials or product-use experience are claimed. The organization byline identifies this project’s research desk; it is not a fictional human reviewer. Read the authorship statement.

What still needs confirmation?

The intended ASIN for each submission, enrolled account eligibility, attribution reports, source conflicts and production measurement hosting. Open the questions register for what is known, what is not and how to find out.
