Meat thermometers: stop guessing.

An instant-read that answers in a second and a wireless probe that stays in the roast, plus the safe temperatures that make either of them worth owning.

Published 2026-11-19

  • Is the turkey done? Where the thermometer goes, and the one number that matters

    165 °F, checked in three places. Where the probe goes, which kind of thermometer makes the day easier, and how to test your thermometer before you trust it.

  • Instant-read or leave-in? Picking a meat thermometer you will actually use

    One lives in a drawer and answers in a second. The other lives in the roast and texts you. They solve different problems, and most kitchens only need one of them.

  • Can you use a meat thermometer in an air fryer?

    Yes, but the kind matters. One of our air fryers halts cooking the moment you open the basket, which changes how you check.
