How should you assess cleanup and counter-space trade-offs?

Measure where it will live, check the removable parts, and separate appliance dimensions from required clearance.

By Counter Notes research deskPublished 19 November 2026Source research · not hands-on testing

Write down the space you have first.

Emeril publishes its appliance dimensions as 16.9 inches deep, 15.8 inches wide and 14.8 inches high. Chefman publishes the two basket models’ dimensions in its own stated order. Keep that order with the source instead of silently assigning width/depth labels the page does not use. Emeril Everyday · specifications · Chefman · dual-window specifications · Chefman · stainless-steel specifications.

Our practical check: Measure the intended location, then consult the exact model’s manual for ventilation and opening requirements. The external appliance footprint is not the same as a verified safe installation envelope.

Read care instructions at the part level.

Chefman calls the dual-window baskets nonstick and dishwasher-safe; its stainless-steel page calls the basket top-rack dishwasher-safe. Those statements concern the named parts. They do not establish that an entire appliance is dishwasher-safe. Chefman · dual-window specifications · Chefman · stainless-steel specifications.

Questions worth settling now

  • What room is required for the door or basket to open?
  • What clearance does the matching current manual specify?
  • Which exact removable parts are covered by the care claim?
  • Will you still want the appliance there after cooking and cleanup?

We have not performed a cleaning-time or counter-clearance test. Required ventilation values and actual cleanup times are not filled in from an appliance’s dimensions or a marketing adjective.
