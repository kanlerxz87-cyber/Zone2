How to judge cleanup and counter space before you buy

The machine you return is rarely the one that cooked badly. It is the one that did not fit under the cabinet or that nobody wanted to wash. Measure these first.

Counter Notes · published 2026-11-19 · sources re-read 2026-11-19 · how we check

Most kitchen-appliance regret is not about cooking. It is about the gap between the top of the machine and the bottom of the cabinet, or the basket nobody wants to scrub. Check these two things before anything else.

1. Will it fit?

Take a tape measure to three places: counter depth, height under the wall cabinet, and the space in front for a drawer or door to open. The official dimensions are below. None of the pages states how far a drawer pulls out, so that row is a dash: measure the one in the shop or ask the seller.
