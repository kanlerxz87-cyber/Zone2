What should you ask before buying a multi-function kitchen appliance?

A decision checklist for a multi-function countertop appliance: your real jobs, model identity, accessories, space and unresolved claims.

By Counter Notes · Published 19 November 2026
AI-assisted and source-led. Not a hands-on test or a measured trend.

THE SHORT ANSWER

List the jobs you actually need, then check the exact model's modes, accessories and instructions. A mode count is not evidence that one appliance replaces everything you own.

Write your own job description first.

Before reading a product's mode count, write down what you want to do with it. Be specific enough to know what would count as a useful answer. Reheating a particular meal, preparing a tray, or managing two dishes are better shopping prompts than a general wish for more versatility.

The Emeril page describes a 10-in-1 appliance and names Air Fry, Bake, Broil, Defrost, Dehydrate, Grill, Proof, Reheat, Roast and Warm. That is a list of manufacturer-described functions, not a test that every function meets your expectations.

Source context: Emeril Lagasse · M3 ↗

Treat inconsistent counts as a question.

The same Emeril manufacturer page presents 25 cooking presets in a specification tile and 24 one-touch presets in the description. Its description also mentions seven accessories, while the Amazon title we observed mentions six. We have not selected whichever number makes the better headline.

Before ordering, ask which model, version and accessory bundle you will receive. A date-stamped conflict is more useful than a confident number that hides the disagreement. The current comparison leaves these disputed fields unverified.

Source context: Emeril Lagasse · M3 ↗ Amazon.com · M4 ↗

Make sure the version is the one you compared.

Model identity matters outside the oven category too. Chefman's stainless-steel page states a 200–400 °F temperature range for the model family, while the observed 8-quart Amazon title advertises a 450 °F Hi-Fry option. These public pages do not give us enough evidence to explain away the difference.

Record the model number shown at purchase and compare the official instructions for that version. Until that is settled, do not use the disputed temperature as the reason to choose it.

Source context: Chefman · M5 ↗ Amazon.com · M6 ↗

Do not retire another appliance on a slogan.

A multi-function claim is a reason to investigate, not our evidence that your existing equipment is redundant. List which jobs you would actually move, what accessories each needs, and what information would make you change your mind.

Our editorial suggestion is to choose a format only after the practical questions—capacity, access, care and placement—have answers. We have not tested replacement performance, energy savings or cooking speed, so none is presented here as a buying benefit we measured.
