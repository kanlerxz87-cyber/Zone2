“Constrained” is not a failure. It means the brief asked for something Amazon's own terms forbid doing literally — usually publishing price data, rates, or automated scraping. In each case the compliant alternative was built instead, and the rule that forced the change is cited.

Disposition of every requirement
IDRequirementStatus What was deliveredSource
R01Automate product research.constrainedA reproducible, source-linked capture process: every figure traces to an official page, and the schema in data/products.json is the input contract for any future automation.
Constraint: Amazon's Agent Terms require automated clients to self-identify as 'Agent/[agent name]', never to disguise automated access, and never to solve or bypass CAPTCHAs. Scraping the storefront is therefore not a lawful automation path.
IP Licence, section 4 Agents
R02Identify trending and high-converting products.not deliverablePoint-in-time signals captured but explicitly not published as rank truth: Amazon's Choice badge and '3K+ bought in past month' on B08DKYBTPH, and a Best Sellers Rank of #28,731 in Home / #49 in Air Fryers on B09B7SB46R.
Constraint: Conversion rate and trend are Amazon-internal metrics. An associate sees only their own click and order reports after login, and those cover the associate's own traffic only. No official public source exists for another seller's conversion, so no such figure is stated.
Commission Income Statement §6 (reporting is per-associate)
R03Analyse pricing.constrainedA price-display compliance rule set, and a documented price snapshot held in the audit trail but withheld from the published tables.
Constraint: A site may show prices only if Amazon serves the link displaying them, or if the data comes from the Creators API / PA API. Any displayed price additionally needs a timestamp and the standard 'prices are accurate as of' disclaimer.
Participation Requirements §2(b); IP Licence §2(i)
R04Analyse reviews.constrainedReview figures captured per storefront with the variation documented.
Constraint: Ratings and review counts are storefront-specific for the same ASIN — 4.5/16,128 on Amazon.co.uk against 4.6/8,361 on Amazon.sa for B09B7SB46R. Quoting one figure as 'the' rating would be misleading, so none is published. Reviews also may not be displayed in a way that alters Program Content.
Participation Requirements §6(a)
R05Analyse competition.deliveredA same-page, same-category comparison drawn only from listings Amazon itself surfaced beside the supplied ASINs, with each comparison product's own ASIN and its own listing as the source.
Constraint: Third-party sales volume, market share and competitor revenue are not published by Amazon and are not estimated here.
Comparison carousels on the product page
R06Analyse commission potential.constrainedA transparent commission model that takes the category rate as an input variable rather than asserting a number.
Constraint: The authoritative rate card is behind an Associates login and could not be read. Amazon's public page states only 'Earn up to 10%'. Reporting a specific category rate from a blog would be a hallucination.
Rate card requires sign-in
R07Generate SEO-optimised product articles, comparisons and buying guides.deliveredA buying guide, an honest comparison, and per-product pages, all generated from the verified dataset rather than free text. Only claims present in data/products.json can appear on a page.
Constraint: No keyword-volume or ranking claims are made; no official public source for those numbers is available to this project.
Participation Requirements §1 (original content)
R08Publish and manage affiliate links.constrainedCanonical ASIN links published directly, with the disclosure placed beside them.
Constraint: Every Special Link must carry the Associate's own tracking ID as a URL parameter. No Store ID was supplied, and inventing one would break attribution and breach §2(b). The short links were also not embedded, because an intermediate redirect that forwards the user without a click disqualifies the purchase.
Participation Requirements §2(b); Commission Income Statement §2(e)
R09Track clicks and conversions.owner decisionNothing tracked, by design. The site loads no analytics, sets no cookies, and embeds no third-party scripts.
Constraint: Click and conversion reporting is only available inside an Associates account, which is owned by the account holder. Client-side tracking of the site's own outbound clicks would also require a cookie/privacy notice under Participation Requirements §3(e).
Participation Requirements §3(e)
R10Continuously optimise campaigns to maximise affiliate revenue.constrainedA documented refresh cadence, a source-diff procedure, and CI that fails the build on an unsourced row or a broken link.
Constraint: Optimisation requires the account holder's own reports. Amazon also requires site content to be recent — generally within 60 days — so the refresh cadence is a compliance obligation, not only an optimisation tactic.
Application review process

Constraint: Automation stops exactly where Amazon's Agent Terms begin.
IP Licence, section 4
