The path from suggestion to change, on this board. 1 — a suggestion arrives as a row with options, costs and risks. 2 — you pick a letter on this page. 3 — the next session writes the letter, your words and the date into docs/review-board.json; the gate fails if the letter is not one the row offered. 4 — only then does an implementation session start, on its own branch, with its own gates. 5 — the row keeps showing the recorded decision instead of vanishing. Nothing skips a step, and nothing is implemented while a row reads “awaiting your decision”.

3. How to reply

Short form is fine. “PS-05 A, PS-02 A, defer PS-07” is a complete answer. Nothing else is required — no form, no file, no account.

What happens next. The decision is written into docs/review-board.json with your words and the date, the page is regenerated, and the gate checks that the option you named is one the row actually offered. Only then does a build session start.

Deferring is an answer. DEFERRED needs one sentence on what would make it decidable. Nothing gets deleted and nothing gets decided for you.
