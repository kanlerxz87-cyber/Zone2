Compliance: the rules this site is built around

Most affiliate sites treat the Associates rules as fine print. This one treats them as constraints that shaped the build — they are the reason you see no prices here, no star ratings, no shortened links, and the same plain disclosure sentence at the foot of every page. The rules below are quoted from Amazon's own documents, re-read live on 2026-11-15.

Affiliate disclosure: As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases.

Operating Agreement §5 — read verbatim, 2026-11-15

The disclosure

"You must clearly and prominently state the following … 'As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases.'" Every page of this site carries that sentence. A violation of §5 is deemed a material breach of the Agreement, and §2 lets Amazon permanently cease payment of commission income without notice.

Read §5 on the official page

Participation Requirements §6 — re-verified 2026-11-14, page updated April 14, 2026

The four rules with teeth here

  • Prices: price display is limited to what Amazon serves; a static page would go stale immediately, so this site prints none.
  • No price tracking or alerting (§6(y)) — which is why the "pricing analysis" module is scoped to published program rates, not per-ASIN price watching.
  • No link shortening in a way that obscures the Amazon destination (§6(w)) — an awkward fact for a brief whose links are already short-link codes.
  • Reviews and star ratings only via Amazon's sanctioned APIs — none are published here.
