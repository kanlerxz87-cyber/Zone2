Article template (used for assets 1–5)

  1. URL — short, descriptive, lowercase-hyphen slug (Google: "Use descriptive URLs").
  2. Title tag / H1 — accurate and descriptive of the page content; main keyword appears naturally (Google: "Influence your title links").
  3. Meta description — short summary of the page (controls the snippet shown in search) (Google: "Control your snippets").
  4. Disclosure block — immediately under the title: "As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases." (OA §5) and "(paid link)"-style labels at each affiliate link (Amazon disclosure help, FTC Disclosures 101).
  5. Key-specs table — one row per attribute, every row linked to the official product page (Google: "Link to relevant resources").
  6. Pros / cons — derived only from official specs and verified reviews; no invented claims (FTC: endorsements must be honest and not misleading).
  7. Who it's for / who should skip — decision guidance in plain language.
  8. Affiliate CTA — "Check price on Amazon" button using the official link format with Tracking ID (link format), labeled as an affiliate link.
  9. FAQ section — 3–5 Q&As matching how people search (Google: "Expect your readers' search terms").
  10. Images — descriptive alt text near relevant text (Google: image guidance).
  11. Disclosure repeat — the Associate statement also appears in the page footer (site-wide requirement).
