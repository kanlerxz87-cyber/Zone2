What cannot be decided from the information that exists

  • Nothing about money. There is no Associates account and no tag, so no click, conversion or earnings figure exists or could exist. No proposal is costed in commission terms, because that would be an invented number.
  • Nothing about demand. Amazon publishes a hard prerequisite for catalog access — enrolment plus at least 10 qualifying sales in the past 30 days — and no analytics or Search Console data exists to substitute. Which products trend cannot be known here.
  • Nothing about the owner's intent. Whether the three supplied links point at the intended products, who the author of record is, which marketplace applies, and whether a domain will be bought are all owner decisions, recorded as open questions rather than assumed.
  • What can be decided now: the editorial and disclosure items. They need no Amazon account, and they are the ones that address the complaint that the site reads like machine output.
