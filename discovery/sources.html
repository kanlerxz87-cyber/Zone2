Associates Program Participation Requirements, Section 6(t) - customer reviews and star ratings section: Associates Program Participation Requirements

(t) You will not display or otherwise use any of our customer reviews or star ratings, in part or in whole, on your Site unless you have obtained a link to that customer review or star rating through Creators API or PA API and you comply with the requirements for Creators API and PA API described in the License.

Associates Program IP License and Usage Requirements - Section 1(a), Section 2(h) and Section 2(k) section: Associates Program IP License and Usage Requirements

we hereby grant you a limited, revocable, non-transferable, non-sublicensable, non-exclusive, royalty-free license to: (a) copy and display Program Content solely on your Site ... (h) You will not store or cache Product Advertising Content consisting of an image, but you may store a link to Product Advertising Content consisting of an image for up to 24 hours. ... (k) If you display Product Advertising Content consisting of text on your application, you will include the following disclaimer in plain view to end users of your application: "CERTAIN CONTENT THAT APPEARS [IN THIS APPLICATION or ON THIS SITE, as applicable] COMES FROM AMAZON. Used for: Product photographs may come only from Program Content, may not be stored or cached (a link to one may be, for up to 24 hours), and displaying Amazon-sourced text requires the plain-view 2(k) disclaimer, which no page on this site carries today. This is the licence constraint behind DG-01, DG-14 and suggestion SG-12.

Google Search Essentials - Spam policies, 'Scaled content abuse' section: scaled-content

Scaled content abuse is when many pages are generated for the primary purpose of manipulating search rankings and not helping users. This abusive practice is typically focused on creating large amounts of unoriginal content that provides little to no value to users, no matter how it's created. ... Using generative AI tools or other similar tools to generate many pages without adding value for users

Used for: Caps any 'publish more pages' growth plan unless every page adds something a competitor's page does not have. Source for DG-03 and suggestion SG-08.

Google Search Central
Google Search Essentials - Spam policies, 'Thin affiliation' section: thin-affiliate-pages

Thin affiliation is the practice of publishing content with product affiliate links where the product descriptions and reviews are copied directly from the original merchant without any original content or added value. ... Good affiliate sites add value by offering meaningful content or features. Examples of good affiliate pages include offering additional information about price, original product reviews, rigorous testing and ratings, navigation of products or categories, and product comparisons.

Used for: Names the four things this site already owns (comparison tables, per-row sourcing, cross-source conflicts, navigation) as the assets a growth plan should build on. Source for DG-03 and suggestion SG-09.
