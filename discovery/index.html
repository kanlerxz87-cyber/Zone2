What this site already does right

It is worth stating plainly, because the fix list below is long and the foundation is not broken.

  • Every value is sourced or blank. 48 specification rows, each ending in a link to the manufacturer or listing page it was read from, with blanks published as blanks rather than guessed.
  • The disclosure is in the right place. The exact required sentence, plus a link-level (paid link) tag beside every product link — which is what the FTC asks for and what most affiliate sites get wrong.
  • Affiliate links are qualified. Every Amazon link carries rel="sponsored nofollow", and the build fails if one does not.
  • It publishes real disagreements. Four specification conflicts between official pages are shown with both values, instead of one being quietly dropped. That is the kind of thing Google's guidance calls added value, and it is the best raw material on the site.

How to read the rest

  • What we found — 17 measured findings, each with the command used to measure it, what it costs, and the official page that governs it.
  • Open questions — 10 pieces of information that do not exist in the repository, with exactly how to obtain each one. No answer is filled in for you.
  • Suggestions — 14 proposals. Each states its options, what each option costs, the evidence behind it and the questions blocking it. Pick one per row.
  • Sources — every official page this review read, with the exact text it relied on, quoted so you can check it without trusting this site.

The short version

The fastest revenue gain is not a design change: it is an Associates ID. Until one exists, every product link on this site earns nothing, and two of the fixes a reader would notice first — a product photograph and a price — are blocked by Amazon's own licence and price rules rather than by effort. The fastest credible gain is the opposite: six pages that answer six buying questions using the specifications the site already holds, plus a measurement layer that says which page sends people where.

Both of those are choices for you, not for this review. They are written up as DS-02, DS-03 and DS-11.
