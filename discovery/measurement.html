Measurement principles

Engineering rules and official context—not a blanket legal assurance.
IDRule and limitationOfficial source
MP-01The counter stores no reader identifier, cookie, IP/UA field, raw referrer or per-user reporting ID.

Amazon's Participation Requirements forbid associating a sub-tag or reporting ID with a specific end user (R-08 in ledger/compliance.json; S02 in ledger/sources.json). A design that cannot do that even if asked is stronger than a policy that says not to.

S46 · AmazonAssociates Program Participation Requirements, Sections 6(v) and 6(w)
MP-02Measure the page and the link, never the person.

Daily page/link counters are descriptive event aggregates, not people or eligible sessions. Authorized account-level orders remain separate and cannot be assigned to individual pages or queries.

S46 · AmazonAssociates Program Participation Requirements, Sections 6(v) and 6(w)
MP-03Publish no traffic, conversion or income expectation, and no forecast.

The Operating Agreement states that Amazon makes no representation or warranty regarding the amount of traffic or commission income an Associate can expect (S58). A projection on this site would present as knowledge exactly what that clause disclaims. The layer records what happened; it never prints what will.

S58 · AmazonAssociates Program Operating Agreement, Section 4 - no representation
MP-04Every event has a stated decision it supports, or it is not collected.

Measurement without a decision is cost with no benefit. This file therefore lists the decision beside each event, and the gate refuses an event with no decision.

Deployment and report gaps

  • MI-01 Writable collector implemented and runnable in a sandbox; permanent authorized hosting is absent. Pages stays OFF. Limits Production EV-01, EV-02, EV-03.
  • MI-02 90-day expiry implemented; daily pruning, backup expiry and infrastructure logging configuration remain deployment requirements. Limits Production retention assurance.
  • MI-03 Trusted browser interactions, strict dimensions and a global request budget reduce noise but are not bot detection. Counts are not unique humans. Limits Human/session-level interpretations.
  • MI-04 Only authorized Associates reports establish reported orders. No report supplied; no page/query attribution inferred. Limits EV-04.
  • MI-05 Verified Search Console property and reviewed authorized query export unavailable. Limits EV-05.

Privacy and consent, stated carefully

Engineering safeguards: aggregate daily counts, no reader identifiers, DNT/GPC honored, no browser queue or retries, 90-day retention. Pages collection remains disabled.

Only the described implementation. Infrastructure sees network metadata; proxy/CDN logging and backups require explicit review. Optional operator proposal preferences use localStorage only on explicit selection and are not counter data.

No blanket GDPR/ePrivacy/CCPA compliance or consent exemption is claimed. Operator identity, jurisdiction, privacy contact and production hosting must be reviewed.

The earlier decision DS-11 remains traceable. Current hosting alternatives are reviewable on S63-02; selecting an option does not provision infrastructure.
