Campaign & Tracking Setup

Attribution model (verified)

  • A Session begins when a customer clicks a Special Link and ends at the first of: 24 hours elapse, an order is placed, or the customer clicks a different Associate's Special Link.
  • Cart purchases qualify if the product is added and the order is completed no later than 89 days after the initial click-through.
  • Commission income is paid approximately 60 days after the end of the month in which it was earned.
  • All Special Links must carry the assigned Associates ID / tag parameter for accurate tracking, reporting and commission accrual.

Source: Associates Program Policies — Commission Income Statement.
