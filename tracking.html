Links & tracking — designed, not yet live

Amazon pays on Special Links: properly tagged link formats carrying your Associates ID. The three supplied short links carry per-link tracking tokens but no Associates tag (F-24) — so as supplied, a reader could click every one of them and the account would still record nothing. This page is the plan that fixes that the day the account and tag exist.

The tracking plan, row by row

Each planned campaign with its link setup and current state. When the Associates tag lands (F-07), the "planned" rows below become the launch checklist — nothing here needs redesigning, only tagging.

How attribution actually works

A reader clicks a Special Link; a 24-hour session starts (longer for items that land in the cart — 89 days to complete the order, per the published Commission Income Statement). Qualifying purchases inside that session are attributed to the tag on the link. No tag, no attribution — which is exactly the gap in the supplied links.

What we will not build

No click-fraud tricks, no cloaked redirects, no cookie-stuffing, no unsolicited email campaigns. Solicited email and SMS are permitted under Program Policies §4, but the site's web-only default stays in place until the owner asks otherwise. And per §6(y), the platform deliberately has no price-tracking or price-alerting feature.
