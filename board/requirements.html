Reading the trace

Most rows hold up. The site is in better shape than a reader of an unincorporated status column would guess: every one of the 171 published pages carries the required disclosure, the front page genuinely leads with the products, and the campaign ledger is exactly the empty-but-defined thing the brief asks for.

Four rows do not hold up, and three of them fail the same way — a status word that says Implemented where the tree shows something partial:

  • S-04 (7 pages with no meta description, 3 duplicated titles) — the only unfinished P1 row.
  • S-19 — no chooser component exists, though a question-led article section does.
  • S-24 — 4 reader pages are one hop short of the row's requirement.
  • S-09 is honestly marked Partly implemented; the missing half is the social preview image, which is the half the row names first.

The loudest number on this page is the one that is not a defect: 2452 Amazon-host links of which 172 carry rel="sponsored". The unmarked remainder are citations to a source inside a verification table, not offers — and Google defines the value by what the link is. See BS-04.
