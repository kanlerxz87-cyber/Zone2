BS-01 · not chosen

Put a real Associates tag on every product link

This is the one row that gates the revenue half of the project. 11 of 177 published pages carry a real tag; none of the five supplied links does either (BQ2-16). Amazon states the tags 'must be embedded in your Amazon product links on the site(s) declared in your application' (BD-13), and that an application is reviewed only after three qualifying sales in 180 days (BD-13). No tag means no attributed purchase, which means no review.

Option A
Test one link first, then scale

Build one tagged Special Link, in the format Amazon publishes, for one product. Click it yourself from the live site. Confirm it lands on the right listing and that the click appears in the Associates reports. Only then rebuild the rest.

Effort
An hour, once the ID exists

Risk
Lowest. A wrong tag is caught on one link instead of on all of them.

Reversibility
Fully reversible — one link

BD-12 BD-13

Option B
Rebuild all five at once

Generate the tagged form for all five products and republish every page that links to them.

Effort
Same hour, plus the rebuild

Risk
Higher. If the marketplace is wrong (BQ2-02), all five links are wrong at once and none of them attributes.

Reversibility
Reversible, but the failure is silent — the links work, they just earn nothing

BD-12 BD-13

Option C
Keep publishing the supplied short links

Leave the links as supplied and continue.

Effort
None

Risk
Highest. Their compliance status is unresolved (BQ2-04), no tag is attached (BQ2-16), and no sale can be attributed. This is the current state, not a plan.

Reversibility
n/a — it is the status quo

How to use this board

Work top-down. The rows are ordered roughly by what each choice unblocks, so choosing the first before the second avoids doing the expensive step twice. Rows blocked by an open question cannot be chosen sensibly yet — the question pages say who can close each one and what it costs to leave it open.

The lean on each row is this session's reading of where the measurements point. It is an argument, not a decision, and it is labelled as one.
