Count what happened. Never invent what sold.

Production deployment gate: open

What is deliberately not measured

No reader ID, cookie, fingerprint, per-user sub-tag, raw referrer, search string or IP/user-agent field. Daily aggregates expire after 90 days with the documented prune task. Infrastructure logging and legal review remain explicit deployment gates. Counters cannot reconstruct a person’s path, deduplicate people, prove bots are excluded, or prove that a disclosure preceded an individual purchase.

Conversion and query importers reject browser submissions, unknown fields, unreviewed queries, future dates and overlapping periods. They never calculate a profitability score or revenue forecast. Source attestation is an operator assertion, not independent authentication of a report.
