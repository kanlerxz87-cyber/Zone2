The 9 things Amazon actually says

Quoted verbatim from S23 on 2026-11-15, so every row below traces to a sentence rather than to a summary:

  • “After you sign up, our Associates team will check your application once you've driven qualified sales (we require at least three within the first 180 days). Please note that personal orders do not qualify.”
  • “All Sites must have robust original content (even when advertising is removed) - a good rule of thumb is at least 10 posts.”
  • “They must be publicly available (for example, they should not be a closed group, or behind a paywall).”
  • “Content on your website must be recent (generally within the last 60 days). You must own your website.”
  • “Associates special links are the identifiers (e.g. abcd-20) embedded in Amazon product links. These tags must be embedded in your Amazon product links on the site(s) declared in your application.”
  • “Unsuitable Sites cannot participate under any circumstance.”
  • “When reviewing a website we look for a beneficial reason as to why a customer would seek out your site.”
  • “Please also remember to keep your Site list updated so that we are reviewing the right traffic sources.”
  • “Your application must clearly list your social media page's exact URL”

What this board does not claim

  • It does not predict whether Amazon will approve an application. S23 states criteria and a reviewer's judgement; a row marked MET is a fact about this repository, not an approval.
  • It does not state a product price, a traffic figure, a conversion rate or a commission estimate. None of those exists in this repository, and the build fails if a price is printed.
  • It does not decide which of the published surfaces is the declared Site (RR-11). That is an owner decision, recorded as open.
  • RR-02 and RR-12 are UNVERIFIED and OPEN because S23 states the ownership requirement without defining how it applies to a github.io address. This board does not fill that gap with an assumption.
